The reference is an AI render. These traces follow the visible silhouettes. They are not a claim that a real font file exists behind the image.

No free face matched. Highest score was **Antonio** (previously-considered) at mean IoU 0.832 with width error 0.172. That is a measurement, not a recommendation.

Previously considered faces (Teko, Antonio, Rajdhani, Oswald, Bebas Neue) are in `06_FONT_COMPARISONS/scores.json`. They are measured, not recommended.

Source-traced glyphs: 38. Derived digits 4–9: 6. Mean trace IoU 0.999.

Comparison faces are SIL Open Font License and were not copied into this pack. No commercial font file was downloaded. Traced SVGs are reconstructions of the founder-supplied AI reference, not a released font. A distributable OTF was not built: the numerals are fixed artwork, and an AI render does not clear the design of an underlying typeface. Do not ship these vectors as a font until that is reviewed.
