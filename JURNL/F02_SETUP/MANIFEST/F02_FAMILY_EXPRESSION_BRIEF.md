# F02 SETUP — family expression

Setup asks what shape a life has, and what JURNL may hold.

The family has four rooms. Arrival is the same doorway as entry: arch, curtain, bust, bowl, books. That repetition is the threshold, and it stops there. The working room is a travertine desk, a closed burgundy book, a brass tray, and one olive sprig. Edit is a stone ledge. Quiet is a lower, warmer pier.

Forms stay on the open left. Amounts are typed, not charted. Skip and permission are sheets.

Today does not get this doorway again.

Global JURNL stays: voice, type, palette, square-rounded controls, natural light.

Cross-family plate reuse: no. The arrival likeness is a continuation of the entry space, not a copied file.

Founder gate: brief ready, distinctness pass, repetition pass, reuse justified for the doorway only.
