# TERRITORY 02 — THE PLAIN ANSWER

![T02 reference candidate](REFERENCE_CANDIDATES/F09_T02_THE_PLAIN_ANSWER_MOBILE_393x852.png)

**TERRITORY ID** `JURNL.F09.T02` · status IN_REVIEW · founder verdict PENDING

**CONCEPT NAME** THE PLAIN ANSWER

**ONE-SENTENCE PREMISE** The page is one plain sentence that answers the question, set into stone, and every held-back thing in it is a word you can open.

## Source-truth derivation

**Brand principles**
- Voice QUIETLY ASSURED + SMART / HUMAN.
- Clarity overrides poetic ambiguity.
- Display serif + tracked sans, all uppercase.
- Travertine.
- Editorial restraint.

**F09 contract facts**
- Brief: *"One word or figure. Wide tracking. Short."*, *"The answer is the parent"*, *"a single mark"*.
- Language layers: function label → state / task → editorial. *"The question never carries primary meaning."*
- Zero bands hidden: the sentence names only non-zero fields.
- Canonical COMPLETE line *"WHAT YOU CAN SPEND NOW WITHOUT TOUCHING BILLS, PLANS OR WHAT YOU’RE HOLDING"*. That line already *is* a sentence; this territory makes it the page.

**User decision.** The user asked a question in words. JURNL answers in words, and the figure is part of the answer.

**State logic.** Completeness changes the grammar: *YOU CAN SPEND* / *ABOUT* / *NOT ENOUGH IS KNOWN YET TO SAY* / *YOU ARE OVER BY*.

**What was invented here (not upstream)**
- The generated-sentence grammar.
- Inline disclosure of amounts on held-back words.
- Cutting the answer *into* travertine (inscription).

## The page

**PRIMARY OBJECT** THE ANSWER SENTENCE, generated from the breakdown, with the figure as its second line.

**PRIMARY COMPOSITION** A left-aligned reading column inside the centred CENTER_STAGE field:

> YOU CAN SPEND / **$24,885** / *(one rule)* / NOW, AFTER BILLS, YOUR PLAN, GOALS AND TRIPS.

The reading follows the sentence. The breakdown and inline actions anchor the lower stage. The family question is a footnote.

**MAJOR ZONES**
1. FUNCTION LABEL (SAFE TO SPEND)
2. THE SENTENCE: lead · figure · single proportion rule (OF $X CASH) · after-clause of openable words
3. READING: a state tile and completeness in plain words
4. SEE THE FULL BREAKDOWN
5. INLINE ACTIONS: CHANGE WHAT’S HELD · PLAN
6. EDITORIAL FOOTNOTE: — WHAT CAN I SPEND WITHOUT UNDOING THE PLAN?

**CORE HIERARCHY**
1. Figure.
2. Lead words.
3. The after-clause.
4. Rule.
5. Reading.
6. Breakdown.
7. Inline actions.
8. Question.

**PRIMARY METRIC / STATE TREATMENT**
- The figure is emerald display serif, 104 px on phone, as the sentence's second line.
- The single mark is one hairline under it: solid emerald for `value ÷ cash` of its length, dotted taupe for the held remainder, captioned OF $X CASH.
- Completeness is a short closing sentence with a state tile (A FULL READING. EVERY BILL HAS AN AMOUNT.).

**PROTECTED / RESERVED INFORMATION TREATMENT**
- Each non-zero held-back field is one *word* in a fixed order: BILLS · YOUR PLAN · GOALS · TRIPS · PURCHASES · WHAT YOU HOLD · YOUR BUFFER.
- Each word carries a champagne underline: it is openable.
- Tapping a word opens its amount inline as a compact emerald chip (shown: YOUR PLAN → $2,400 ASSIGNED).
- Protected and buffer read as the user's own words (WHAT YOU HOLD, YOUR BUFFER), distinct from obligations.

**CTA HIERARCHY**
1. **SEE THE FULL BREAKDOWN**: full-width emerald primary.
2. **CHANGE WHAT’S HELD**: inline action.
3. **PLAN**: inline action.

Chrome and nav are unchanged. The openable words are tertiary, in-sentence controls.

**INTERACTION RHYTHM**
- **Read top-down.**
- **Open one word.** The amount appears where the word stands and the sentence reflows. Only one word is open at a time.
- **SEE THE FULL BREAKDOWN.** The whole reading.
- **CHANGE WHAT’S HELD.** The sheet rises.
- **No in-page navigation** for a single factor. The answer stays the parent.

**ASK JURNL PLACEMENT LOGIC**
- Global chrome (info).
- Ask is the natural follow-up to a spoken answer, but it is consent-gated and explanation-only, so it stays out of the sentence.
- A sentence must never end in a control that answers UNAVAILABLE.

**QUICK ADD RELATIONSHIP**
- Nav + (MOVEMENT).
- After a save, the figure word re-sets in place, with no count-up. The proportion rule re-measures.
- The new sentence is announced by a polite live region (missing today).

**BACKGROUND / PERIMETER STRATEGY**
- The whole ground is pale travertine: low-frequency banding and pores, with soft window light from the upper left.
- Dappled olive *shade* (not an object) falls only across the outer frame, upper left and lower right.
- The answer is visually cut into the stone with a light / dark edge, so the material and the message are one surface.

**EDITORIAL ROLE**
- Footnote.
- The family question appears once, small, after the actions, introduced by an em dash.
- The sentence is functional copy, not editorial. It must stay plain: no metaphors.

**MATERIAL / SURFACE LANGUAGE**
- Travertine ground.
- Inscribed display serif.
- Champagne underlines.
- Emerald figure, chips and primary.
- No panels and no cards.

**MOTION / TRANSITION IDEA** (concept only)
- Still.
- A word opens with a width ease of the chip, and the sentence reflows without jumping lines where possible.
- A recalculation cross-fades the figure word.
- The hold sheet rises.

## Why

**WHY THIS IS SPECIFICALLY JURNL** The voice is the brand's most specific asset: quietly assured, smart, human, direct. This territory makes the voice the composition. It is serif-led, uppercase and restrained, cut into JURNL stone.

**WHY THIS IS SPECIFICALLY SAFE TO SPEND**
- It is the only JURNL family whose whole job is a one-sentence answer to one question.
- The sentence structure, *you can spend X, after these*, is the formula read aloud.
- F03 previews the figure. F09 states the *answer with its conditions*.

**HOW IT DIFFERS FROM THE OTHER TWO**
- **Linguistic and linear.**
- T01 encodes held-back as area in a drawing; T03 as sealed objects.
- Here they are words in a grammar.
- Entry is the top-left of a reading column, not a centre or an object.
- Interaction is inline disclosure, not spatial inspection or object manipulation.

**BLUR-TEST RESULT: PASS.** With travertine and shade removed, the page is still one large sentence. The figure is inside it, with openable held-back words, a single proportion rule and a reading line. It is identifiable as the answer to *what can I spend* by structure alone.

**LEGACY VISUAL DEPENDENCIES: NONE.** One note for the founder: its single proportion rule is the closest echo of the legacy threshold line. It is horizontal, sits inline in a sentence and carries no bands. It answers the canonical brief's *"a single mark"*.
