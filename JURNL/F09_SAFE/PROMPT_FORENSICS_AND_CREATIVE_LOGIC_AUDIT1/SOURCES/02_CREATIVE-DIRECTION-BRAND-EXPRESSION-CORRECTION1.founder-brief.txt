==================================================
CONVERSATION CONTEXT / WHY THIS SPRINT EXISTS
==================================================

The previous sprint:

P0.JURNL.F09-SAFE-TO-SPEND.VISUAL-AUTHORITY-3-TERRITORY-PROOF1

successfully produced three structurally distinct F09 SAFE TO SPEND territories:

01 THE OPEN FLOOR
02 THE PLAIN ANSWER
03 THE OPEN ENVELOPE

and proved:

- LEGACY_VISUAL_LEAK = 0
- 3/3 distinct territories
- blur-test PASS
- no implementation changes
- no production changes
- strong product/data grounding
- correct family distinction

However, founder review identified a critical methodology gap.

The three concepts were structurally cohesive, but they did NOT contain enough of JURNL’s complete visual identity, graphic-design richness, bespoke art direction, or branded world-building character.

They read as:

GOOD PRODUCT CONCEPTS
+
CLEAN UI STUDIES

rather than:

FULLY CREATIVE-DIRECTED JURNL AUTHORITY CANDIDATES

The founder identified that the pipeline currently jumps too directly from:

UPSTREAM CONTRACTS
→ STRUCTURAL TERRITORIES
→ REFERENCE CANDIDATES

without a sufficient intermediate:

CREATIVE DIRECTION / BRAND EXPRESSION TRANSLATION GATE

This caused several deficiencies:

- insufficient environmental richness
- insufficient tactile/material richness
- insufficient graphic-design authorship
- insufficient bespoke visual composition
- weak integration of JURNL’s full brand identity
- outdated/underdeveloped logo presentation
- insufficient art-direction logic
- insufficient use of custom-designed visual objects
- visual results that risk reading as AI-generated design studies
- reference candidates rendered through local HTML/CSS/SVG instead of the preferred high-fidelity image-generation workflow
- no Sunburst / 4K image-generation pass
- incomplete translation from product logic to art-directed branded expression

This sprint corrects that methodology gap.
