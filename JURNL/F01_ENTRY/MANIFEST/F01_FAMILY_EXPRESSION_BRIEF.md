# F01 ENTRY — family expression

Entry opens the house. The question is whether a person can come in and trust the place.

Welcome is the atrium: side light, plaster, arch, curtain, bust, books, and a dark bowl. That arrangement belongs to this threshold. The other entry screens keep their own quieter plates. Forms stay sparse. There is no money chart.

Setup may pass through this doorway, then it has to become its own rooms. Do not hand this still life to a later family.

Global JURNL stays: voice, type, palette, square-rounded controls, natural light, and a plaster field for the words.

Cross-family plate reuse: no.

Founder gate: brief ready, distinctness pass, repetition pass, reuse none.
