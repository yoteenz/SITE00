# F04 ACTIVITY — family expression

Activity answers what happened, when, and for how much. It is a record, not a second today.

The room is a reading wall. Plaster takes most of the frame. Light crosses it. The coast is a sliver. There is no bust and no bowl. A small stack of books may sit low. The ledger is ivory strips on one paper surface. The amount stays inside the row.

Search is a mode: the query, a human summary, then matches or a designed empty state. Detail is a sheet in the same voice.

This must not look like today with the figure removed, and it must not look like the entry doorway.

Global JURNL stays: voice, type, palette, square-rounded controls, and the money formatter.
