## The founder utility buttons (U01–U04)

The handoff's CLOSE, MENU and ADD files have identical alpha to the OPUS1 masters, with RGB within 1/255. SEARCH is byte-identical. All four are already in the runtime (OPUS1). The colour-fringe check at mobile scale passes:
- residual red or yellow rim pixels have alpha 1–4 / 255;
- their largest visible deviation over olive is 3.3 / 255;
- the alpha ≤ 2 edge dust was cleared in OPUS1.

On the MONEY parent, SEARCH, ADD and MENU keep their order, trigger ids and actions. The founder CLOSE is mounted on the seven cream / gold close authorities, with no extra circle or rectangular backing.
