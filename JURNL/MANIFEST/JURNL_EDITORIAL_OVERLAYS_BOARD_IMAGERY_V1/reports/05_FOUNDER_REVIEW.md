## Why PARTIAL (not pixel-identical)

* **Content is unchanged, as asked.** Where the live form or list is longer than the board's concept (02, 03, 05, 07) the
  folio is taller than drawn. The board's own composition is kept at its top, and its foot is anchored to the bottom.
* **Board-only subjects are soft.** The medallions, wax seal, clip, ledgers, coin stack, columns, coin, amphora, drapes and
  landscapes exist only on the 1448 × 1086 board. They are lifted at 2× (Lanczos), which is about 3× upsampled on a
  DSF 3 phone, so they look softer than the SK01 / V9 art. A higher-resolution export of the board would make them
  crisp, through the same script and the same rects.
* **Type metrics.** The runtime serif is wider than the board's lettering, so titles are 5–10 % smaller at the drawn width.
* **06 layout.** It keeps the F09 reference-replica layout (lockup, ACCOUNT title, every card at its reference box).
  Only its imagery follows board 06. The replica test now asserts board 06's parts instead of the superseded F09 grey
  shell; every control and geometry assertion is unchanged.
* **08.** No JURNL tag, because it is text. **03.** No plaster wall, because there is no approved plaster source.
  **04** stays anchored to its pocket.
