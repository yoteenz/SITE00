# PROTECTED OPEN FIELDS

Production systems treat **OPEN** as OPEN — no inference.

| Resident | Field | Status |
|----------|-------|--------|
| EV | sexuality, partner | OPEN (`evSexuality`, `evPartner`) |
| Marlowe | husband identity | OPEN |
| Zuri | partner identity | OPEN |
| Noa | wife / daughters identities | OPEN |
| Iona | future relationship | OPEN |
| Etta | current partner | OPEN |
| Caspian | current partner | OPEN |

Validated in dossiers via `protectedOpenFields` arrays. Cast-role overrides cannot close these via `validateCastRoleOverridesForResident()`.
