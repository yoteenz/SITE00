# 10 — Founder executive summary

## Bottom line

NDXBOOK / Studio World is a **serious creative production engine** for **short-form branded entries** (especially NDX Entry 002): narrative synthesis, founder gates, storyboards, character continuity architecture, film planning, and provider guardrails. It is **not yet** a turnkey engine for **THE EXCHANGE** — a separate serialized, three-part scripted property — without **new property isolation + durable fiction canon + episode/part modeling**.

## What you can reuse soon

- **Project capability system** — add a new slug without giving it NDX’s Entry 002 baggage.  
- **Founder approval culture** — LOVE_IT gates, spend authorization, reference binding patterns.  
- **Character continuity + casting** — Sloane’s approved look can live as **Exchange-project** references and locks (when you authorize production).  
- **Film rough-cut planning** — structural edit workflow, not final render farm.

## What is not ready

- **Engine-hosted Opus series review** — blocked until bibles persist and prompts are Exchange-scoped.  
- **Three-part episode pipeline** — not in the codebase.  
- **Automatic Kling reel delivery** — routing exists; dispatch is off; Kling 4 work is unmerged.  
- **“Series bible in the cloud”** for fiction — brand/identity lore exists; fiction ontology does not.
