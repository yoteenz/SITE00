# 04 — Season 1 directorial bible

**Status:** PROVISIONAL — FOUNDER REVIEW REQUIRED. The season story structure is approved. This bible is not, and approving it would not approve any script, performance, shot or music cue.

**How to use it.** The bible keeps 86 releases coherent; it is not a template. Each scene still chooses its own camera, light, music and rhythm in its directorial plan, and labels each choice. A warm family dinner should not look or sound like a tense institutional meeting, and an Exchange Night should not be shot like a private confession. Where this says "usually", a scene may break it with a reason on record. The machine-checkable rules are the entries in `THE_EXCHANGE/SEASON_1/PRODUCTION_KNOWLEDGE_BASE_V1.json` (PK-001 to PK-014); this document carries the reasoning.

**Labels.** `SOURCE_ESTABLISHED`: stated in founder source, the approved season master or a founder directive. `DIRECTORIAL_INTERPRETATION`: a choice made here that serves the source and can be changed. `FOUNDER_APPROVAL_REQUIRED`: open until the founder decides.

## 1. Cinematic identity

- **SOURCE_ESTABLISHED** — A nostalgic-futuristic, emotionally alive, privacy-first parallel present: expressive Black 1980s and 1990s cultural richness in advanced contemporary materials. Not literal period costume, sterile cyberpunk or stagey futuristic exposition. *(MB-02)*
- **SOURCE_ESTABLISHED** — Two registers. Public social scenes are fluid, vibrant and layered, with spontaneous reactions. Intimate rooms are patient, warm, observant and emotionally focused. *(MB-02, filmmaking grammar)*
- **SOURCE_ESTABLISHED** — Keep glamour, humour, romance, social intrigue, joy and vulnerability in balance. Do not drift into an institution explainer, a conspiracy thriller, a procedural or a networking drama. *(NR)* Do not imitate prestige drama by making everything dark, slow or distant. *(V2 §25, PK-013)*
- **SOURCE_ESTABLISHED** — Early releases show joy, people, Sloane and one novel norm or device at a time; understanding comes later. *(MB-08, progressive disclosure)*
- **DIRECTORIAL_INTERPRETATION** — The camera is an affectionate guest: close enough to enjoy the room, never a surveillance lens. In a world where capture needs consent, the series' own camera avoids hidden-camera, CCTV and phone-screen grammar unless capture is the subject of the scene, as with the neighbour in S1-01.

## 2. Visual motifs

- **SOURCE_ESTABLISHED** — Copper and gold are the Ember's two states: copper is *no*, gold is *yes* *(M S1-58)*. Copper appears first unexplained on a lens (S1-01), then in sealing wax (S1-17), on a card's edge (S1-44) and in lanterns (S1-59, S1-67, S1-77). Gold is consented capture (S1-30, S1-75), the river (S1-80, S1-81) and the lantern in Celestine's window (S1-86).
- **DIRECTORIAL_INTERPRETATION** — Reserve true copper and true gold light for the Ember and its objects. Grade every other warm source (tungsten, candles, amber windows, brass) so it cannot be mistaken for either. S1-01 already applies this: amber, not gold, in the ballroom windows; copper must not read as gold.
- **FOUNDER_APPROVAL_REQUIRED** — The Ember's on-lens look is not designed (FD-S101-03). Whatever is approved becomes the season reference for every copper and gold appearance.
- **SOURCE_ESTABLISHED** — Objects that recur across arcs: the brass Pledge token stamped 1986, Relay cards, the *C. R.* envelope, dance cards, Ada's ledger, the fern and the tomato plant, copper and gold lanterns. *(Approved map, collected in R12 §5)*
- **DIRECTORIAL_INTERPRETATION** — Hands carry the theme of access given person to person: terms delivered by hand (S1-17), a blank card passed round a sewing circle (S1-34), a glass raised across a street (S1-01). When the story is a handoff, the hands get their own insert. *(Handoff coverage is founder direction, PK-005.)*
- **DIRECTORIAL_INTERPRETATION** — Parallels are planned, not invented: S1-01 ends on lit windows across the rooftops and S1-86 ends on a gold lantern in a window with the city beyond it. Both images are in the master; pairing their framing is a choice.

## 3. Colour and lighting

- **SOURCE_ESTABLISHED** — Jewel tones, marigold, plum, emerald, red and cobalt; saturated social lighting; black lacquer, walnut, sculptural glass, chrome, brushed brass and amber illumination. Technology is warm, tactile and quiet, never default blue holograms. *(MB-02)*
- **DIRECTORIAL_INTERPRETATION** — Light follows the social temperature, not one season grade:
  - Nights: saturated, several coloured practicals, people lit by the room.
  - Family rooms: daylight and tungsten, lived-in and a little untidy.
  - Institutional rooms (the registry, offices): fewer sources and cleaner lines, still walnut and brass, so they read as composure, not menace.
  - Confessions and grief: closer and softer, but faces stay readable. A scene is dark only when its time of day calls for it.
- **DIRECTORIAL_INTERPRETATION** — Light Black skin first: warm bounce and a controlled sheen rather than flat fill. No grade may crush darker complexions for mood.
- **DIRECTORIAL_INTERPRETATION** — Blue hour and night lean blue-violet and cobalt; no teal-and-orange by default.
- **FOUNDER_APPROVAL_REQUIRED** — One approved reference still per register before the first principal release (S1-02).

## 4. Fashion coverage

- **SOURCE_ESTABLISHED** — Sculpted tailoring, bold jewellery, striking silhouettes and real, lived-in diversity; not endless identical luxury settings. *(MB-02)*
- **SOURCE_ESTABLISHED** — A gown reveal is its own floor-level shot, not a by-product of a wider move. *(PK-003)*
- **SOURCE_ESTABLISHED** — Clothes are tested by movement: *"If I can't dance in it, it's a costume"* *(M S1-02; also S1-62, S1-72)*.
- **SOURCE_ESTABLISHED** — Wardrobe anchors in the approved map: Wendell Ames's orange suit (S1-06, S1-28), the 1986 cropped jacket (S1-20), a gown with sneakers (S1-40), Richard's tuxedo and the cane he takes "for style" (S1-71), Mavis's church hats, Dee's 1985 relay jacket over sequins. *(Collected in R12 §5)*
- **DIRECTORIAL_INTERPRETATION** — When an outfit matters, cover it in up to three beats: silhouette (full figure, with headroom for the vertical frame), material (fabric moving: a lining, beading, a hem) and behaviour (the outfit doing something: dancing, sitting, coming off a hanger). Not every outfit gets all three.
- **DIRECTORIAL_INTERPRETATION** — One look per character per fictional day. *(Recommended in R12 §2; the WARDROBE continuity check enforces it once a look is registered.)*
- **FOUNDER_APPROVAL_REQUIRED** — No wardrobe is designed yet. Each anchor needs a locked reference before its first release; S1-01's jewel-green dress is FD-S101-05, and Sloane's wardrobe language is open (U-4).

## 7. Exchange Night treatment

- **SOURCE_ESTABLISHED** — Legendary parties people dream of attending, not networking conferences in disguise: music, fashion, beautiful environments, hospitality, food, dancing, humour, flirtation, spontaneous encounters. The audience should want to attend even if no transaction happens. Sloane visibly enjoys hosting. *(XN)*
- **SOURCE_ESTABLISHED** — Crowd as plate, principals as pockets; no more than two speaking faces per shot. *(PK-010, approved direction)*
- **DIRECTORIAL_INTERPRETATION** — Shoot Nights in the public register: a moving camera, layered foregrounds, people crossing the frame, reactions caught away from the main action. Map each venue as three fixed pockets (bar, floor, edge) plus one hero position, identical across releases in that venue. *(R12 §3)*
- **DIRECTORIAL_INTERPRETATION** — Dance through inserts and silhouettes (feet, hands, skirts, shoulders, a silhouette against the band) rather than full-body partner choreography in one generated shot. *(R12 §3; one primary action per risky shot, PK-001)*
- **DIRECTORIAL_INTERPRETATION** — Light is the spectacle: the Echo Track (S1-40), the Hush circle (S1-50) and the lanterns (S1-77) are set pieces, and cheaper and more controllable than more crowd. *(R12 §3)*
- **DIRECTORIAL_INTERPRETATION** — Every Night is different. *Nineteen Eighty-Six* is a dress code and an orchestra; *The Breakfast Dance* is evening clothes on a misty track at dawn; *The Hush* is music you step into and out of; the Grand Exchange is the river and the lanterns. Give each Night one signature image and one signature sound, and never reuse a Night's opening shot.
- **FOUNDER_APPROVAL_REQUIRED** — Event names and frequency are provisional. *(XN)*

## 8. Music supervision

- **SOURCE_ESTABLISHED** — House, R&B, funk, soul, jazz and warmer synth textures, with original contemporary evolutions. Silence is a musical decision. *(MB-02, PK-014)*
- **SOURCE_ESTABLISHED** — Narration falls silent when performance carries the moment: grief, intimacy, vulnerability, family revelations, confrontations, decisions. *(NFG)*
- **DIRECTORIAL_INTERPRETATION** — Score backs off in those same moments. Let the room's sound carry them.
- **DIRECTORIAL_INTERPRETATION** — Prefer music that exists in the world (bands, a record player, a radio, an orchestra) over wall-to-wall score. Live performance is part of this world's freedom *(MB-02)*, so the camera should often find the musicians.
- **DIRECTORIAL_INTERPRETATION** — Genre follows the room: jazz and big band at Halcyon and the Crescent Room, soul and R&B at home, house where the night runs late, warm synths for the Ember and the inventions. Each scene still picks its own cue.
- Engine rule — every cue records its rights requirement and route, and nothing is treated as cleared (deliverable 14).
- **FOUNDER_APPROVAL_REQUIRED** — Composer route and any house theme (FD-S101-02).

## 9. Recurring musical motifs

- **SOURCE_ESTABLISHED** — Music already in the story: the Crescent Room Orchestra (S1-09, S1-20, S1-28), Noelle's song (S1-35, S1-74), the Hush pockets (S1-50), the breakfast-dance band (S1-40), the record player in the garage memory *(M S1-58; R07)*.
- **DIRECTORIAL_INTERPRETATION** — S1-01 proposes a Halcyon House big-band figure (cue MX03 "Halcyon") heard from across the rooftops. It could become the house theme that returns whenever the story walks back through those doors. Not approved.
- **DIRECTORIAL_INTERPRETATION** — Motifs travel by instrument and figure, not by quoting the whole tune every time; a few notes on a trumpet at a doorway are enough.
- **FOUNDER_APPROVAL_REQUIRED** — S1-01 treats the Ember as silent: the music stops and no effect is designed. Keeping the Ember silent all season, so that its absence of sound becomes its signature, is a season decision for the founder.

## 10. Sound design language

- **SOURCE_ESTABLISHED** — Technology is quiet *(MB-02)*: no beeps, whooshes or interface chirps for the inventions.
- **SOURCE_ESTABLISHED** — Dialogue is one continuous audio event; picture may cut away and return. Lip-sync only where a speaking mouth is visible. *(PK-009)*
- **DIRECTORIAL_INTERPRETATION** — Every recurring room has its own ambience: the street bed, the ballroom's walla and band bleed, Richard's kitchen (pans, a radio), the registry's paper and clock. Design each once and reuse it with the set.
- **DIRECTORIAL_INTERPRETATION** — Foley carries fashion and touch: fabric, jewellery, glass, paper. S1-01 uses eight foley cues, all on hands, fabric and glass.
- **DIRECTORIAL_INTERPRETATION** — Restraint: one designed sound per moment. Before an emotional line, take ambience away rather than add a sting.

## 11. Scene transitions

- **SOURCE_ESTABLISHED** — Complex interactions are split into deliberate coverage, one primary action per risky shot. *(PK-001)* Freeze-frame introductions are elegant, never cartoonish, and only for selected characters. *(NFG)*
- Engine rule — inserts, reactions, freeze cards and transitions are separate shots, never squeezed into one long generation (founder-canon-lock-v1 deliverable 05).
- **DIRECTORIAL_INTERPRETATION** — Cuts are motivated by an action, a look or a sound: S1-01 cuts on a pat on the glass, on a grin, on a glass going up. Use match cuts and continued moves for real parallels (SH17 into SH18). Fades are rare: the start or end of a release, and black only when the music needs the last word.
- **DIRECTORIAL_INTERPRETATION** — Between the public and intimate registers, let sound lead the picture: the party bleeds into the quiet room for a beat, or the quiet arrives a beat before the cut.
- **FOUNDER_APPROVAL_REQUIRED** — Title-card and freeze-card design for the season (S1-01's end card is FD-S101-10).

## 12. Opening and closing rhythm

- **SOURCE_ESTABLISHED** — The first episode opens on a joyful, colourful, socially uninhibited scene where recording norms are visible but not lectured. Each part gives a real payoff, not just a teaser, and Part 3 resolves rather than leaving an endless cliffhanger. *(MB-08)* Every release in the master has an opening hook and an ending transition.
- **DIRECTORIAL_INTERPRETATION** — Open on an action already under way that poses a question within the first two seconds, not an establishing shot that waits. Close on a held image or a line that leaves warmth or a question, then cut.
- **DIRECTORIAL_INTERPRETATION** — Vary the shape. Releases run from 40 seconds to three and a half minutes; short ones are one movement, longer ones get a breath in the middle. Not every release ends on someone looking out of a window.

## 13. Social-first framing

- **SOURCE_ESTABLISHED** — S1-01 is vertical 9:16. *(V2 §28)* Part 1 is not published before Parts 2 and 3 are finished and checked. *(MB-08)*
- **DIRECTORIAL_INTERPRETATION** — Compose for height: stack foreground and background vertically (balconies, staircases, a dress over a rail). Two-shots are diagonal over-the-shoulders rather than side by side.
- **DIRECTORIAL_INTERPRETATION** — Keep faces and key action in the middle band, clear of platform captions and buttons at the top and bottom. The first beat reads with the sound off; meaningful sound gets an accessibility subtitle (S1-01 has four).
- **FOUNDER_APPROVAL_REQUIRED** — Whether every release is 9:16, or some are also delivered in another ratio.

## 14. Long-form continuity

- **SOURCE_ESTABLISHED** — Writers'-room truth stays separate from audience knowledge, and reveals are checked. *(PL-C-VIVIENNE, approved canon)* Fifteen continuity checks (ages, chronology, the Ember rule, wardrobe, props, reveals and more) run on every build: 0 hard conflicts today. *(`CONTINUITY_VALIDATION_REPORT_V1.json`)*
- **SOURCE_ESTABLISHED** — Do not build future cast assets without an immediate production reason. *(V2 §27)* Future needs are tracked silently (deliverable 25).
- **DIRECTORIAL_INTERPRETATION** — Every approved image becomes a reference. Once a face, room, prop or light state is approved, later shots must cite it (keyframe provenance and spatial authority records).
- **DIRECTORIAL_INTERPRETATION** — Plant images that pay off later, and record both ends in the dependency graph: S1-01's copper lens pays off in S1-58.
