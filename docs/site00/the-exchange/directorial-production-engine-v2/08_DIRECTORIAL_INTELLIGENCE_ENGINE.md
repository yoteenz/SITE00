## What is implemented

The engine is code with tests. A director (the agent, then the founder) authors a scene plan; the engine derives every timecode, track, event, cost and review element from it and refuses a plan that breaks a rule. It does not write a directed scene by itself: plans for future releases are authored when each release becomes active.

## Flow

Season master → scene index (Level A) → active release selected → directed scene authored → validation → ten-section packet → founder review steps → generation gate (nothing dispatches in this sprint) → edit → founder final approval → archive → next release.

| G | Point of view | An affectionate secret witness: the camera sees each private moment from a respectful distance, then takes the neighbour's point of view once, at the moment he gives up the recording. |
| H | Visual dramaturgy | Each inhabitant performs one private action alone; nobody shares a frame until the toast crosses the street. Mostly locked off for the vignettes so the gestures carry; the camera only moves to open (tilt down) and to close (rise and push). Dusk compressed from golden to blue across the release; copper is the sun at the start and only the Ember at blue hour. Everyday clothes on everyone except the jewel-green dress, which is the one garment dressed for the night. Brownstones, fire escapes and a balcony across from a window; geography held so the toast reads across a real street. Vignettes of varied length, cuts on action and on beats, a held silence on the Ember, then one continuous rise to black. |
| I | Atmosphere | Warm, funny and unhurried: the last minute of daylight in a neighbourhood where people still dress up and still look out for each other. |
| J | Sonic identity | A big band warming up out of sight becomes the groove of the street, stops dead on the Ember, and returns as the real band heard from the ballroom. |
| K | Ending impression | The audience should want to go where everyone is going: across the rooftops to the lit windows of Halcyon House, on a night that is about to begin. |

Intent text that leans on filler (cinematic, epic, stunning, breathtaking, gorgeous, visually striking…) fails validation: every field must say what is actually seen or heard.
