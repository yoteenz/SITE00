## Bands

Founder-approved guidelines (sprint V2 §09). A shot outside its band needs a reason on record; a silent exception is an error.

| Class | Screen time | Typical content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HIGH | 1–3 s | walking details, fashion movement, gown reveals, prop handoffs, physical gestures, fast choreography |
| MODERATE | 2–4 s | turning, standing up, reaching, opening doors, picking up objects |
| LOW | 2+ s | seated conversation, listening, stationary close-ups, controlled dialogue |
| EMOTIONAL_DIALOGUE | 3+ s | confessions, revelations, intimate exchanges, monologues, uninterrupted reactions |
| ENVIRONMENT | 2–6 s | establishing moves, crane rises, architecture reveals |

**Generation length.** Requested seconds = screen time + 0.5 s handle, rounded up, clamped to 3–15 s (the kling-3-omni form accepts whole seconds only).
**One action.** A high-motion generated shot carries one primary physical action. A gesture that needs two is split into coverage (PK-001).
