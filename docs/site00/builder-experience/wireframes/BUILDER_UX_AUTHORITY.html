Templates provide grammar, not identity. Clients react to structures and expressions they can see. The Builder assembles a Blueprint from those reactions, and the canonical estimator turns it into a projected window and investment. Family units, lanes and coefficients never reach the client.

W07 · FEATURES

Verbs, with consequences in words

W10 · DELIVERY

Priority only where it helps

  1. The estimator decides feasibility for this exact configuration. With estimator 1.0.0, priority shortens only large projects (about 10+ production families).
  2. When it cannot shorten the project, it is shown as not available and nothing is charged (founder decision F4).
  3. The relative effect (“about 30% sooner”) comes from the estimator. Windows and figures appear only at the estimate.
  4. Never “pay double, half the time”. The “why not half” card explains why.

W11 · BLUEPRINT

A drawing, not an order summary

  1. The client finally sees structure and style together: their schematic rendered in their expression.

  3. Open decisions sit on top. “See the estimate” stays disabled until none remain, so there is never an estimate for an impossible project.
