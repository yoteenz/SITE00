# Pricing communication recommendation

**Output 19.** No public price was changed and no estimator value was changed.

**Evidence:** `BUILDER_PRICING_EVIDENCE.json`, generated from estimator version 1.0.0 by `scripts/site00/builder-experience-export.ts`.

## 1. The discrepancy

There are three different price systems today.

**Public BLDR anchors** (`bldr-classification.ts`, `builder.ts`, `bldr-entry.ts`):
- SITE: FROM $4K+
- WORLD: FROM $10K+
- ENTERPRISE: FROM $25K+

**Historical context:**
- SIMPLE: about $3K
- CUSTOM: about $10K

**Estimator floors** (`assumptions.ts`):
- SIMPLE $3K, which includes 2 family units of production
- ADVANCED $6K, which includes 5
- CUSTOM $10K, which includes 8
- above the included scope: $1,800 per extra unit (marked calibration-needed)

The three systems use **different axes**:
- The public anchors are by **build kind**: site / world / enterprise.
- The historical prices and the estimator floors are by **build level**: simple / advanced / custom.

So none of them can simply be swapped for another.

**What this shows:**
1. **Site.** The $3K–$4K entry is real, but only for the smallest site. The simple floor includes 2 family units, which is roughly a front door plus one offer page. A *typical* simple site lands at $5K–$8K, so a "$3K" anchor alone sets most simple clients up for a surprise of nearly 2× at the reveal.
2. **World.** The public WORLD anchor ($10K+) is **below** the estimator's smallest world ($14K–$20K refined).
3. **Enterprise.** The public ENTERPRISE anchor ($25K+) is **below** the estimator's portal ($39K–$53K refined).
4. **Upper end.** The large engine examples in the sprint (World $69K–$94K, Large product $43K–$55K) are the upper end of their kinds, not their entry points.

## 2. Options

| Option | What the client sees | Strength | Problem |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** Starting prices publicly + scoped estimate later | "FROM $3K" | Simple, familiar | Most simple clients see 2× at the reveal; WORLD and ENTERPRISE anchors are below what the estimator produces. Trust breaks at the most important moment. |
| **B** Build-level range upfront + refined later | "SIMPLE $3K–$11K" | Honest about spread | Ranges for whole levels are so wide they say little. Price dominates early exploration, against the founder's principle. |
| **C** Starting price for SIMPLE only; scoped for ADVANCED / CUSTOM | "SIMPLE FROM $3K"; others "scoped" | Productises Simple | Gives no signal for World and Systems, which is exactly where the current anchors are most wrong. Still leaves the 2× simple surprise. |
| **D** (recommended) Engine-anchored entry + typical band | "SITE: FROM $3K · MOST SIMPLE SITES $5K–$8K" | Honest anchor, honest expectation, cannot drift | Needs one founder decision per build kind about which reference configuration defines "entry". |

## 3. Recommendation: Option D, engine-anchored entry points

**Public pages (BLDR hub, classification, homepage expanded).** One line per build kind, with two figures:
- **FROM**: the refined-range low of a named, founder-approved *entry reference configuration* for that kind.
- **TYPICAL**: the refined range of a named *typical reference configuration*.

Both figures are **generated by the estimator** from those configurations at each estimator version, the same way `BUILDER_PRICING_EVIDENCE.json` is generated today. Public copy then cannot drift from the estimator again.

**Inside the Builder:**
- **Rooms I–IV:** no money; scope words only.
- **Reveal:** INITIAL RANGE.
- **After Blueprint review:** REFINED RANGE.
- **After that:** the quote.

**Illustrative values at estimator 1.0.0.** These are not proposed copy; final copy needs founder approval.

| Build kind | Entry reference | FROM | Typical reference | TYPICAL |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SITE | Front door + one offer page | ~$3K | Simple practice site | $6K–$7K (refined) |
| WORLD | Few places, look-only, flat | ~$14K | Dimensional showroom | $37K–$50K |
| SYSTEM / ENTERPRISE | Client portal | ~$39K | — (founder to name) | — |

**Why D:**
- It keeps the founder's historical **$3K** start, which is real and honest for a minimal site.
- It stops that number from misleading the typical simple client.
- It exposes the World / Enterprise gap instead of hiding it.
- It never lets a hand-typed price disagree with the estimator.
- Price stays out of creative exploration, which honours the founder's principle.

### Where the base offer ends and scoped production begins

- **Base offer (SIMPLE BUILD, the published entry):**
  - one site grammar
  - one visual system in its **essential edition**, with no secondary influence
  - the front door plus the structure's core experiences at ESSENTIAL depth
  - only capabilities the estimator allows at SIMPLE (for example BOOK, ACCOUNTS, EDIT IT YOURSELF, EMAIL, UPLOAD FILES; TAKE PAYMENT is advanced-only today, see E8)
  - phone + desktop
  - standard delivery
  - 2 review rounds per approval point
- **Within that, the published "FROM" figure** covers the estimator's included scope for SIMPLE (2 family units: a front door plus one offer page). Each further experience is priced by the estimator as scoped production.
- **Scoped production begins** with any of these: a further experience beyond the included scope, an ADVANCED or CUSTOM build (full edition, a secondary influence, an advanced capability, world or system), or a world or system part.

The founder can move this line by calibrating `includedFamilyUnits.SIMPLE` and `buildFloors.SIMPLE` in a calibration sprint (decision F2). This sprint did not change them.
