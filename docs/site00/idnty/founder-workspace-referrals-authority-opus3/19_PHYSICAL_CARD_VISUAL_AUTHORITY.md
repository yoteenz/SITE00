# 19 · Physical card visual authority

Boards: R29 (fronts + physical mockups + finish table), R30 (backs + pairs), R33–R34 (proof canvas, TEST band). All artwork is rendered from `rf-kit.js card()`. The partner shown is **AIO as DESIGN ONLY**: no card for AIO may be printed before FD-R17.

## 1. Shared rules (all territories)
- **Format:** 3.5 × 2 in landscape (US business card), single partner per card, front + back.
- **Type:**
  - SITE 00 wordmark in Saira Semi Condensed 600, tracked 0.26 em;
  - `DIGITAL FOUNDATION.` in SITE 00 Display 900 with the red period;
  - service and back copy in Saira Semi Condensed, uppercase, minimum 6 pt printed.
- **Colour:** paper white / `#FBFAF8`, ink `#0C0C0C`, SITE 00 red `#CE1017`. No gradients on type; no other accent.
- **Imagery:** only approved SITE 00 environment photographs (pavilion, doorway, red-glass tall). Never the reference image; never partner logos until supplied.
- **QR:** black on a white panel with a 4-module quiet zone, on the back only, inside the safe area. Until validated it is the placeholder labelled NOT VALID FOR PRINT.
- **Back hierarchy:** call to action → explanation → URL (code masked in all specimens) → INTRODUCED BY → partner name.

## 2. Territory A · ARCHITECTURAL EDITORIAL
- **Front:** red-glass pavilion photograph over the left 46% (full-bleed on three edges), with the type block on the right.
- **Back:** white field, QR right, a thin red-glass photo strip on the right edge.
- **Character:** closest to the reference mockup; warm, architectural, premium.
- **Proposed finish:** 16 pt matte with a soft-touch laminate on the front.
- **Complexity: MEDIUM** — full-bleed photo; colour-managed red needs a proof.

## 3. Territory B · MINIMAL ACCESS CARD
- **Front:** pure white, `SITE 00`, large `DIGITAL FOUNDATION.`, service lines, one short red rule.
- **Back:** white, one red rule at the top, QR right.
- **Character:** quiet, typographic, the most legible, and the cheapest to reprint.
- **Proposed finish:** 32 pt uncoated duplex, with optional red edge paint.
- **Complexity: LOW–MEDIUM** — edge paint adds a vendor step.

## 4. Territory C · RED STRUCTURAL SIGNATURE
- **Front:** a red structural column (left 11%, full height) plus a tall red-glass photograph on the right 27%, with black type between.
- **Back:** solid SITE 00 red with white type; the QR on a white panel.
- **Character:** the strongest brand signature; unmistakable in a stack of cards.
- **Proposed finish:** 16 pt matte with spot red (Pantone match to be proofed against `#CE1017`).
- **Complexity: MEDIUM–HIGH** — a large solid red needs a press proof, and rich black is avoided on red.

## 5. Physical mockups
Board R29 §02 shows each territory as a front-and-back pair, tilted on a neutral surface (CSS perspective; no photographic compositing). The mockups indicate scale and finish only; they are not production previews.
