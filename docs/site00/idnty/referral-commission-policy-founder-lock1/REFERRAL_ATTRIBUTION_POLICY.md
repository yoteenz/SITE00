# Referral attribution policy

## Window — FD-R07

**90 days** from valid partner touch to qualifying artifact attribution.

## Durability

When a qualifying intake artifact is created, accepted attribution is **persisted on the client record**. Later cookie expiry does **not** remove accepted attribution.

## Collisions — FD-R08

Multiple partner touches → **manual founder review** (ATTRIBUTION EXCEPTION). Do not auto-switch first/last touch. **One commission** per qualifying base-package purchase.

## Disqualifiers — FD-R09

| Case | Treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Self-referral | DISQUALIFIED — record reason |
| Duplicate referral for same purchase | DISQUALIFIED |
| Existing client (repeat purchase only) | No **new** commission; preserve prior attribution |
| Related party / ambiguous | FOUNDER REVIEW |

## Confidentiality (FD-R09 companion — partner privacy)

Partner compensation rates and dollar amounts are **not** shown on public landing pages, client intake, marketing pages, or printed cards (see `REFERRAL_CARD_POLICY_ALIGNMENT.md`).
