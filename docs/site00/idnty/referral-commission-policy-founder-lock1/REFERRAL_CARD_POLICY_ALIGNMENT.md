# Referral card policy alignment

Separates **physical card creative** (FD-R13/R14 partial) from **commission confidentiality** (FD-R03 / partner privacy).

## Approved creative direction (not print-approved)

| Surface | Copy / treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Front | **YOUR BUSINESS. BUILT TO LAST.** |
| Back headline | **START HERE.** |
| Back body | QR-driven intake entry · four concise service benefits · partner attribution · **All In One Enterprises Inc.** in red |
| Finish | Reflective red metallic foil on selected red typography and accents |

## Must not appear on cards

- Commission rates or dollar amounts  
- Partner tier labels tied to compensation  
- “15%” (rejected reference imagery) or other percentage offers  

## Production gates

- Do not generate production QR artwork until partner attribution URLs are **durable and verified** (Opus3 doc 21)  
- Do not mark **PRINT READY** from mockup alone — physical proof and production spec remain gated (V-09)  
- AIO cards remain **DESIGN ONLY** until FD-R17 activation requirements satisfied  

## Commerce alignment

Card drives **intake entry** only; compensation follows **verified $500 base payment** + policy lock rules, not card distribution alone.
