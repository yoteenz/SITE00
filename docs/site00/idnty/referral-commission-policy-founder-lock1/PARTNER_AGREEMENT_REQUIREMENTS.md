# Partner agreement requirements

Activation and compensation require documented terms **before** partner status becomes ACTIVE (reinforces FD-R17, FD-R10 direction).

## Terms must define

- Partner identity  
- **Fixed referral fee** (minor units, ≤ 5000 cap unless policy amended)  
- Qualifying product ($500 DF base)  
- Attribution rules (90-day window, collision review)  
- Eligibility (verified payment + 14-day hold)  
- Payment schedule (monthly, 15th target)  
- Refund and dispute handling  
- Termination treatment (FD-R19 when decided)  
- Effective date and **policy version**  
- Agreement acceptance evidence (timestamp, actor, document hash/version)

## Payee documentation (FD-R10 — open detail)

- Secure W-9 or equivalent before recording payout **completed**  
- Do not expose taxpayer data in client artifacts or public surfaces  
- Tax reporting thresholds: **configurable** — do not hardcode 1099 thresholds in code without legal review  

## Confidentiality

Partner-specific fee amounts appear only on **authorized founder and partner** surfaces — never on cards, public landings, or client intake (FD-R09 / card alignment docs).
