# SITE 00 Correspondence V2: three-email editorial benchmark

Start here. This package rebuilds three emails from the ground up after the V1 lean pack (PR #1603) was rejected for copy that repeated itself and narrated internal rules to the customer.

Nothing in this package has been sent, merged, or deployed. No OpenArt credits were spent. The other seventeen templates have not been touched and will not be until you approve these three.

## What to look at first

1. `CONTACT_SHEET/SITE00_EMAIL_V2_BENCHMARK_CONTACT_SHEET.jpg`: all three emails side by side, desktop and mobile.
2. `RENDERS/DESKTOP/` and `RENDERS/MOBILE/`: each email at full resolution (2x), 600px desktop column and 390px phone.
3. `COPY/`: the words of each email on their own, with subject, preheader, and every field.

## The three emails

| | Email | What it should feel like | Shape |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Personal foundation invitation | Personal attention, welcome, trust | A letter from the studio, with one quiet doorway image and one action: begin the intake |
| 02 | Foundation complete | Accomplishment, ownership, confidence | A milestone record: a drawn foundation slab, a five-line ledger of what was laid, and a plain statement that the accounts are yours |
| 03 | M06 — BLDR introduction | Curiosity, ambition, discovery | An editorial feature: a full-width hero, a three-part walk through the building, three illustrative plans, one invitation |

They share a typeface, a palette, and a way of speaking. They do not share a layout, an opening, a call to action, or a sentence.

## Questions for your review

- Does each one read as written by a person for this client, not assembled?
- Can you tell the three apart with the words blurred?
- Is there anything in the copy that sounds like a system talking about itself?
- Would you send any of these to a real client today, once the contract gaps in `REVIEW/CONTRACT_GAPS.md` are closed?

Reply with approve, revise (with notes), or reject for each email.
