Status: benchmark draft, pending founder review.

Visual authority: `docs/site00/idnty/visual-authority/marketing-email/SITE00_MARKETING_EMAIL_APPROVED_AUTHORITY_V1.jpg` (Architectural Editorial × Blueprint Dispatch, SHA-256 `e814042c318f7f45d7db07a6d11e4878fcc20888c583a76537828cfa3c2ede83`).

The authority defines the creative language. It does not define one mandatory template.

## Brand canon (fixed in every email)

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| Ground | Luminous white `#FFFFFF`, with `#F6F5F2` stone only as a resting band. |
| Accent | SITE 00 red `#D90C0C`. Used as accent, seal, rule, or single CTA. Each email declares its red budget. |
| Type | Black editorial uppercase, Saira Semi Condensed. Scale per `EDITORIAL_VOICE_SYSTEM.md`. |
| Photography | Photoreal architecture only: marble, concrete, red glass, horizon. Reused from approved plates. |
| Blueprint | Thin line drawing, dimension ticks, registration crosshair. Code-native SVG, exported to PNG for send. |
| Grid | 600px column, 40px desktop margin, 22px mobile margin. Hairlines `#E4E2DD`. |
| Masthead | `SITE 00` wordmark set in type, with a register line. Never baked into an image. |

## Content canon (varies by purpose)

Structure, number of images, image role, presence of numbering, CTA shape, and how much red appears are all decided per email from the editorial intent brief.

## Composition library

| Composition | When | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| PERSONAL LETTER | First contact, invitations, personal access | Letterhead, salutation, prose, inline action, sign-off. Mostly type. One quiet image at most. |
| MILESTONE RECORD | Completion, activation, confirmed payment | One monumental moment, then a ledger of what is true now. Numerals only when they count real things. |
| EDITORIAL FEATURE | Marketing education, BLDR, discovery | Rich hero, lede, a sequence of sections that progress, a revelation beat, a resting image, a full CTA band. |
| REFINED NOTICE | Failure, action required | Status line, one fact, one action. No hero. |
| EXECUTIVE BRIEF | Quotes, scope changes | Figure-led summary, change list, decision. |
| BLUEPRINT EXPLANATION | Education about how something works | Line drawing progression with captions. |

## Image roles

A reused image must take a specific role. Allowed roles: HERO, DETAIL, CROPPED OBSERVATION, TRANSITION, SECTION ANCHOR, COMPARISON, CLOSING IMAGE. An image that cannot hold one of these roles is not used, and the email is marked as a premium art candidate instead.

## Rules

1. No two emails in a send sequence share the same full-page structure.
2. Images sit where the copy needs them. They are never spaced at regular intervals between paragraphs.
3. Numbering appears only when it counts real things in order (delivery stages, a walk through a building).
4. The full-width red CTA band is reserved for FEATURE emails. LETTER uses an inline button. RECORD uses a black or outlined button.
5. Every image has alt text written as description, not as marketing copy.
6. White space is a declared pause, not leftover space.
7. Mobile is recomposed. Each email documents what changes on mobile and why.
8. No full-page image as the email. All words are live text.

## Benchmark block maps

### B01 — Personal Foundation Invitation — PERSONAL LETTER

| Block | Job |
|---|---|
| Letterhead | SITE 00 wordmark, register line, "PREPARED FOR" with the business name. Sets personal attention before a word of the letter is read. |
| Doorway strip (desktop: tall left column) | CROPPED OBSERVATION of the red glass doorway from DF-I01. A door left open. Quiet, narrow, never a hero. |
| Headline | The reason for writing, in one sentence. |
| Letter body | What has been prepared, what the briefing asks, what happens next. |
| Inline action | Red button sized to its words, with the literal link beneath in original case. |
| Sign-off and P.S. | Human close. The P.S. removes the most common hesitation. |
| Footer | Why they received it. No marketing unsubscribe (access message). |

Red budget: one hairline, one registration mark, one button.

Mobile: the doorway becomes a short horizontal band above the letter, re-cropped to keep the glass edge on the left third.

### B02 — Foundation Complete — MILESTONE RECORD

| Block | Job |
|---|---|
| Masthead and status line | `FOUNDATION COMPLETE` as a stamped status, not a banner. |
| Monument | Blueprint drawing of the foundation slab, drawn in line and filled with real marble from DF-I03. The drawing turning into material is the visual expression of "established". |
| Headline and deck | The groundwork is done, and the accounts belong to the client. |
| Ledger | Five delivered stages, each with what it now means for the client. Numbered because they are real stages. |
| Ownership paragraph | Who owns the accounts. |
| Continuity | Where the full record arrives and that the foundation page stays open. |
| Quiet action | Black button. |
| Closing image | CLOSING IMAGE: the terrace edge and horizon from DF-I03. Rest after the record. No caption about BLDR. |
| Footer | Service notice. No marketing unsubscribe. No offer. |

Red budget: the status seal, the slab edge and crosshair, and the headline's full stop. Nothing else.

Mobile: the monument is re-cropped tighter around the slab; the ledger moves from two columns (number and stage left, meaning right) to stacked rows.

| Block | Job |
