# Digital Foundation visual batch protocol

Canonical for batches A–J. Change the screen inventory. Do not change this pipeline.

1. Recover the approved boards in `docs/site00/idnty/df-visual-fidelity-v1/` (REF-01 through REF-07).
2. Read the screen contracts in `shared/site00-digital-foundation/` and the parent manifest.
3. Fill `DF_VISUAL_BATCH_SCHEMA.json` for the batch.
4. Generate each screen as its own OpenArt image, image-to-image from a cropped approved phone. Do not use another provider.
5. Inspect every image. Regenerate only a failed screen, and record why.
6. Export the candidates at their native size. Do not stretch them.
7. Keep the originals. Build one labeled contact sheet. Do not crop the sheet into fake screens.
8. Write the manifest, the prompts, the provenance, and a short review guide.
9. Zip those files. Verify the zip opens and the checksums match.
10. Publish a direct download. The founder reviews the batch.
11. Approval states: PENDING, APPROVED, APPROVED WITH SURGICAL REVISIONS, REVISION REQUIRED, REJECTED.
12. Only an approved reference goes to Opus for implementation.

OpenArt project for these batches: `SITE00 IDNTY Digital Foundation Production` (`6Ueswtr8vt58ur2n3CRs`).

Model: GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst, image-to-image, 9:16, resolution tier 4k, quality high, auto-enhance off. The provider returns 2016×3584 for that tier.

Do not implement the screens, change routes, touch Anthony Gate A, Supabase, Railway, cPanel, Stripe, or send email. Do not start the next batch automatically.

Sequence after A: B records and approvals, C completion and expansion, D founder operations, E runbook execution, F business growth, G communications command, H transactional emails, I marketing emails, J communication details.
