## Safeguards before any paid run

- Founder names the ceiling in writing (recommended 6,340 credits).
- Runner stores the 317-credit quote and stops if a later quote differs.
- Four jobs in flight, matching the only concurrency this account has demonstrated. The provider did not publish a limit.
- Two attempts maximum per asset.
- Existing sha256 skips the job.
- Batch zip after each batch. Master zip at the end.

## Optimization already applied

- No second pass on A, B, or the seven boards.
- No tablet twin of every phone.
- No per-recipient email art.
- No upscale step (4k delivery is the native provider size).
- 2k at 172 credits is cheaper and is **not** recommended; it breaks the approved 2016×3584 baseline.
