# Founder review guide — lean email pack

Status: FOUNDER_REVIEW_PENDING

Twenty Digital Foundation emails. Forty boards: desktop (600px column) and mobile (390px). Same copy on both. OpenArt generations: 0.

## How to read a board

The envelope strip shows SUBJECT and PREHEADER. The red button is the primary action. Tokens such as {{business_name}} and {{quote_total_display}} are not sample client data.

## What to judge

- Does the family feel like SITE 00: white field, condensed black type, red action, thin rules, architectural photography.
- Are the twenty compositions different enough.
- Do transactional messages stay clear, and marketing messages carry the editorial briefing.
- Which templates need a custom plate later. Premium is not authorized in this pack.

## Candidates if premium art is wanted later

Invitation, quote ready, project activated, foundation credit, M06, and M13. Shared photography is intentional on the lean plan.

## Not in this pack

No HTML send templates, no SMTP, no live sends, no production flags changed.
