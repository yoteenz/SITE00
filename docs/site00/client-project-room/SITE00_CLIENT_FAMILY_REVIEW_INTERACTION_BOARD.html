What it proves

Approve needs no comment. It settles into an approved state with a short undo window.

Needs revision never submits on tap. It opens the feedback space in place. Send revision request stays disabled until the note is meaningful: at least 12 characters and 3 words, or one topic chip plus 6 characters.

Prices and payment never appear on this screen. Funding shows up later, as the next step after approval.

States

Spec

Controls

  • ♥ and × are decorative (aria-hidden). The buttons are named "Approve" and "Needs revision".
  • Each button is at least 48px tall and full width on mobile, in the thumb zone. No swipe gestures.
  • Status is always written in words, never shown by colour or symbol alone.

Feedback

  • Prompt: "What isn't working for you?" in a large field.
  • Optional chips in plain language. No design vocabulary required.
  • Validation runs on the client and the server; the server accepts an empty summary today.
  • Drafts autosave. Leaving with text offers KEEP DRAFT or DISCARD.
  • On mobile, a focused sheet keeps the prompt, field and send button above the keyboard.

Confirmation

  • Standard gates: light inline confirmation with UNDO for a short grace window.
  • Final or contractual gates: an explicit confirmation that lists what becomes final.
  • After the window, or once production starts, an approval is history. A change becomes a new version.

Events written

  • APPROVE / NEEDS_REVISION / APPROVAL_UNDONE receipts with family, authority, version, previous and new status, reason and chips.
  • Gate: FAMILY_01_APPROVED → FAMILY_02_READY_TO_PRODUCE (or AWAITING_PRODUCTION_FUNDING).
  • Founder sees it in the production INBOX client lens.
