# Season One Development Slate — 10 Episodes / 30 Parts

**STATUS: ILLUSTRATIVE WORKING CONCEPTS, NOT FOUNDER-APPROVED SCRIPTS.**
Each part below is a potential story function rather than a fixed scene outline. Preserve alternative paths during critique. All episodes must be intelligible without secret lore.

## E01 — The Invitation
**Premise:** Exclusive event access reveals a request beyond status
- **E01P1 — The Request / Hook:** At a colorful social gathering Sloane notices an unusual effort to gain entry; a credible petitioner asks for a single invitation.
- **E01P2 — The Cost / Reversal:** The person whose access is sought has conditions; Sloane discovers the applicant’s public reason is incomplete.
- **E01P3 — The Choice / Resolution:** Sloane arranges a consensual opportunity with an unforeseen personal payoff; no proof she can force entry or acceptance.
- **Subtext / seed:** Playful social Sloane; vibrant privacy culture; access means trust.

## E02 — The Favor
**Premise:** An escalating misunderstanding over who owes whom
- **E02P1 — The Request / Hook:** An apparently easy favor generates contradictory expectations and Sloane agrees to mediate.
- **E02P2 — The Cost / Reversal:** Multiple participants value the same favor differently; an informal obligation risks harming a friendship.
- **E02P3 — The Choice / Resolution:** Sloane restores clarity with agreed terms and lets one person refuse.
- **Subtext / seed:** Nadine gently exposes how Sloane regards helping as habitual.

## E03 — Seven Degrees
**Premise:** A reunion through unlikely human connections
- **E03P1 — The Request / Hook:** Someone asks Sloane to locate a legitimate path to a distant contact, not track a person against their will.
- **E03P2 — The Cost / Reversal:** Every connection fails until a non-elite person with an unlikely history becomes vital.
- **E03P3 — The Choice / Resolution:** The contact consents to an opportunity, and the outcome is not what the requester expected.
- **Subtext / seed:** Father gives delightful advice; generational relational intelligence.

## E04 — The Promise
**Premise:** A decades-old promise proves more complex than literal fulfillment
- **E04P1 — The Request / Hook:** An older petitioner tries to honor an agreement exactly as remembered.
- **E04P2 — The Cost / Reversal:** Parties disagree about original intent and ethics of strict adherence.
- **E04P3 — The Choice / Resolution:** A better consensual act honors meaning rather than a coercive technicality.
- **Subtext / seed:** Sloane’s discomfort with broken promises is visible, not explained.

## E05 — The Collaborator
**Premise:** An equal partner challenges Sloane’s methods
- **E05P1 — The Request / Hook:** An exchange requires external expertise; Sloane and an accomplished collaborator meet in a spirited professional disagreement.
- **E05P2 — The Cost / Reversal:** His solution is viable but requires Sloane to relinquish control; mutual respect emerges.
- **E05P3 — The Choice / Resolution:** They find a compelling joint outcome; no romance announced.
- **Subtext / seed:** First subtle wit/chemistry; collaborator has independent agenda.

## E06 — The Anniversary
**Premise:** An older widower’s tradition intersects with Lockwood grief
- **E06P1 — The Request / Hook:** A man seeks help preserving an annual ritual and involving a reluctant relative.
- **E06P2 — The Cost / Reversal:** A refusal has a legitimate history; Sloane reevaluates whose need the ritual serves.
- **E06P3 — The Choice / Resolution:** Family honors tradition without forced reconciliation. Sloane has a quiet call with her father.
- **Subtext / seed:** Mother acknowledged absent/deceased when earned; avoid exposition.

## E07 — The Exception
**Premise:** A request tests a seemingly settled access rule
- **E07P1 — The Request / Hook:** A credible request enters through an unusual but not necessarily illicit channel.
- **E07P2 — The Cost / Reversal:** The exchange appears eligible yet imposes disproportionate risk to someone not requesting it.
- **E07P3 — The Choice / Resolution:** Sloane can refuse or redesign the terms; a father’s unease remains a small subtext.
- **Subtext / seed:** Demonstrate meaningful limits; tech not a magic permission bypass.

## E08 — No Strings
**Premise:** A simple invitation challenges Sloane’s transactional reflex
- **E08P1 — The Request / Hook:** After a successful exchange the recurring collaborator invites Sloane to dinner for no professional reason.
- **E08P2 — The Cost / Reversal:** Sloane looks for hidden intent while another modest exchange mirrors her difficulty receiving care.
- **E08P3 — The Choice / Resolution:** The case resolves and Sloane accepts a non-transactional human connection, not full romantic commitment.
- **Subtext / seed:** Romance grows through mutual play and everyday attention.

## E09 — The Unfinished Business
**Premise:** A historical obligation touches mother’s generation
- **E09P1 — The Request / Hook:** An elderly petitioner asks to complete unfinished business using authenticated records.
- **E09P2 — The Cost / Reversal:** New context shifts the meaning of the original agreement; a historic connection to mother’s era is discovered.
- **E09P3 — The Choice / Resolution:** Standalone need resolves or reaches an ethical no; one genuinely useful family question emerges.
- **Subtext / seed:** Mother’s influence visible, no complete final-exchange reveal.

## E10 — The Recall
**Premise:** A former obligation reopens under disputed terms
- **E10P1 — The Request / Hook:** A rare priority Recall interrupts normal life and references a previous exchange.
- **E10P2 — The Cost / Reversal:** A recorded settlement differs from what someone believed; technical verification is not human resolution.
- **E10P3 — The Choice / Resolution:** Sloane makes a principled resolution with consequences; her father recognizes a deeper historical pattern.
- **Subtext / seed:** First meaningful season-level connection; promise of answers, not exposition.
