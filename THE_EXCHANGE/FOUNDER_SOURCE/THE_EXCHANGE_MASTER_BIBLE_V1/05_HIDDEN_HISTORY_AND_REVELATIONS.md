# Hidden Family History, Character Belief, Audience Knowledge

## Golden rule
Maintain separate records of **WORLD_TRUTH**, **CHARACTER_BELIEF**, and **AUDIENCE_KNOWLEDGE**. The writer's room may know an eventual truth before viewers do; however, **our current creative development has not yet established the whole truth**. Don't fabricate it and mark it canon.

## Facts / high-confidence selected narrative direction
- Mother pivotal to early private relational systems and ethics, deceased by main timeline.
- Death linked to a consequential 'final exchange'; exact cause unconfirmed.
- Father alive, departed active network after her death, remains Sloane's beloved adviser.
- Two daughters remember their parents and costs differently.
- Sloane's identity centers on competence, protection and relational usefulness.
- An inherited institutional obligation or discontinuity may exist, but its mechanics have not been finalized.

## Suggested revelation curve — ILLUSTRATIVE
**Early:** laugh and enjoy Sloane; privacy is casually normal; noticing she has principles and a complicated family. Distinct episode payoffs, no exposition. A casual phrase or object may recur.
**Middle:** father gives advice, sisters disagree about boundaries, mother is acknowledged as deceased, later a familiar tech or social custom connects to mother's professional ethos. Future romantic collaborator returns without contrivance.
**Late first slate:** first verifiable historical connection or unusual Recall creates a more consequential question without explaining entire final maternal exchange. A small early detail changes meaning.
**Later seasons:** major reveals must answer material questions and alter relationships, not endlessly tease.

## Repeatable foreshadowing devices — OPTIONAL
A vintage ring of uncertain provenance; father's unusual hesitation at a device; an old historical ledger entry; mother-associated phrase; a familiar guest whose sponsor is omitted; an unusual binding restriction; a specific anniversary family avoids. None are locked standalone facts. Do not overpopulate the first ten episodes with clues.

## Avoid
- Mystery-sounding aphorisms in every scene.
- Mother reduced to secret plot coupon; father reduced to liar.
- Clues without later causal payoff.
- Technology functioning differently solely for cliffhangers.
- Everyone conspiring against Sloane.
- Implying a death was deserved or evidence of heroic unconditional love.
- Confusing withheld audience information with uncreated writer's-room truth.

## Core end-state (direction not promised plot)
Learning to honor mother's legacy without reenacting self-sacrifice, a father able to discuss grief, sisters seeing one another outside childhood roles, Sloane choosing presence over compulsory usefulness, and romance as mutual trust. The institution can continue while the family's relationship to it evolves.
