# THE EXCHANGE — Founder Source Package V1.0

**Property:** `the-exchange` | **Purpose:** founder-source ingestion and subsequent independent Opus critique | **Dated:** October 2026

## Status discipline
- **FOUNDER_SELECTED / APPROVED_DIRECTION:** named or expressly endorsed in development conversation. This does not imply that every detail in the section is final.
- **WORKING_CONCEPT:** written explorations not separately approved as hard canon; do not silently promote.
- **UNRESOLVED:** deliberately unanswered; never infer a solution.
- **ILLUSTRATIVE:** examples and episode sketches, not finished scripts.
- **PRODUCTION_PENDING:** cannot claim as rendered, runtime-validated, or deployed.

The words *approved direction* here mean creative selection by the founder, not an application database APPROVED_CANON state. The importing system must require explicit promotion for exact records. This package deliberately defaults records to `WORKING_CONCEPT`, except narrow confirmed identity/format declarations. Preserve source documents unaltered.

## Priority / conflict rules
1. Founder explicit decisions > earlier assistant recommendations.
2. Final selected **Sloane Lockwood** supersedes **Sloane Mercer** (Mercer is used elsewhere in Studio World) and candidate surnames.
3. The Exchange is **independent fictional IP**. NDXBOOK / SITE 00 supplies possible tooling, not fictional canon.
4. Full-family historical truth is **NOT solved**. Mother's final exchange, cause/circumstances of death, beneficiaries, and culpability remain unknown.
5. Ten episode names and three-part concepts are a **development slate**, not approved screenplays or release commitments.
6. Proposed technology names and formats are design candidates pending world-science review.
7. Existing imagery is **only** casting/reference imagery. Do not derive biographical claims from AI-generated text on character boards.
