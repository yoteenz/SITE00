# World Authority — Parallel Present, Social Freedom, Privacy

## Status
The founder strongly selected a **nostalgic-futuristic, emotionally alive, privacy-first parallel world**. Precise calendar year, country-by-country systems, and legal enforcement are WORKING_CONCEPT. Proposed default: **parallel contemporary ~2026** retaining expressive 1980s/1990s visual heritage, with technological divergence beginning decades earlier. In this timeline COVID-19 did not occur; avoid simplistic scientific claim that a pandemic's absence necessarily creates more advanced technology.

## Civilizational thesis
**Connection is essential. Access is conditional.** A civilization favored intentional access, identity verification, trust, discretion and selective disclosure while keeping physical culture alive. People dance, flirt, compete, gossip, argue, party, dress extraordinarily, enjoy live performance and experience events rather than automatically broadcasting them. The public is not emotionally docile; people remain messy, ambitious and funny.

## Cultural social juxtaposition — HIGH PRIORITY FOUNDER DIRECTION
- Seeing someone in person does not equal consent to record or distribute their image.
- Unscripted nightlife and house parties are freer because the risk of secret recording and viral humiliation is substantially reduced.
- Public culture is vibrant; no austere antiseptic future. Rich colors, lively street scenes, expressive fashion, Black social spaces, clubs, hospitality, gallery events, community life.
- Personal access carries weight. Relationships may be private without being hidden, shameful or inauthentic. Giving someone a direct communications channel can be romantic and emotionally significant.
- A person can make a social mistake or grow beyond adolescence without ordinary embarrassing behavior automatically becoming permanent public media.
- Social reputations form through firsthand experiences, introductions and word of mouth, not only metrics.
- People still consent to photography, films, press events and recorded public performances. Public accountability and preservation of evidence for wrongdoing must still function. Do NOT portray inviolable magical anti-camera technology.
- Some people misuse privacy as cover for coercion or inequity. Inequality, exceptions, journalism, exploitation and enforcement are rich story material.

## Proposed everyday communications taxonomy — WORKING_CONCEPT
- **The Common:** public cultural distribution (creative works, events, public figures, entertainment), without automatic access to private individuals.
- **Circles:** mutually authorized private social communication and sharing.
- **Relays:** authenticated, permissioned contact through approved intermediary protocols.
These are candidate infrastructure names; preserve unless reviewed. Not every episode needs to name them.

## Venue protocols — WORKING_CONCEPT
- **Open Capture:** explicitly recordable shows, press rooms, selected public events.
- **Consented Capture:** photography/distribution governed by clear participant permissions.
- **Private Presence:** recording/distribution restricted by technical policy, architecture, social norms and law.
No total camera impossibility. Clear accountability, evidence handling and lawful exceptions to be explored with care.

## Speculative historical milestones — OPEN
Late 1980s: relational computing movement. Early 2000s: public-access/privacy reckoning. 2010s onwards: widespread intentional access protocols. These are useful structural hypotheses, not historical facts to harden without critique.

## Aesthetic authority — founder-selected direction
Expressive Black 1980s/1990s cultural richness reinterpreted in advanced contemporary materials. Jewel tones, marigold, plum, emerald, red, cobalt, saturated social lighting, black lacquer, walnut, sculptural glass, chrome, brushed brass and amber illumination. Fashion: sculpted tailoring, bold jewelry, striking silhouettes, real lived-in diversity; NOT endless identical luxury settings. Tech: warm, tactile, quiet, beautiful, non-generic; not default blue holograms. Sound: house/R&B/funk/soul/jazz/warmer synth textures with original contemporary evolutions. Avoid literal period costume, sterile cyberpunk and stagey futuristic exposition.

## Filmmaking grammar
Public social scenes: fluid, vibrant, immersion, layered action and spontaneous reactions. Intimate rooms: patient, warm, observant and emotionally focused. The viewer sees private events fictionally; that does not mean characters in-world may secretly record or distribute them.

## Introduce through behavior
Suggested illustrative opener: at a joyful party, someone attempts covert capture; venue-device permission policy declines it; guests casually rebuke the recorder and return to dancing. Do not halt scene to explain privacy legislation. The main reason the scene exists is Sloane and an irresistible human encounter.

## World tensions
Privacy vs accountability, wealth disparities in high-end protections, fame without private access, social status via earned trust, sponsors abusing vouching, technology enabling new forms of control, consent withdrawal, archivists and journalists, trust exploitation. This society is neither utopia nor a dystopia.
