# Technology Bible — Working Inventions and Hard Constraints

## Thesis
In the parallel society, computing developed robust systems for discrete identity, selective disclosure, authorized connection, consent and physical/social presence. Extraordinary technologies should feel science-adjacent, physical, design-rich and socially consequential—not vague fantastical powers. **All named devices below are WORKING_CONCEPTS, not automatically final canon.**

| Candidate | What it enables | Hard limit / dramatic counterpoint |
|---|---|---|
| The Relay | Authenticated private conditional communications and invitations | Credential integrity doesn't prove message truth or goodwill |
| The Constellation | Permission-based relational graph and history of introductions/obligations | Doesn't read minds or reveal undocumented links; people may refuse |
| The Veil | Consensual selective disclosure/identity shielding in protected meeting | Can't guarantee honest intent or emotional safety |
| Echo Archive | Authenticated archives linked in spatial chronology | Can't recreate unrecorded memories or consciousness |
| The Pledge / Witness | Verifiable agreement, negotiated terms and authorization context | Can't compel fulfillment or prove sincerity; recording consent required |
| The Threshold | Granular single-purpose access to spaces/experiences | Requires authorization; no bypassing security/third-party autonomy |
| The Interval | Asynchronous contextual presence/messages in controlled environment | Not time travel or communication with dead; only stored, consented content |

## Research/engineering questions for Opus
Explain distinct feasible scientific foundations vs speculative leaps; technical prerequisites, standardization and cost; who manufactures/maintains devices; failures and spoofing risks; whether privacy controls can be abused; legal exceptions and accountability; how public/private permissions intersect with journalism; technologies that are commonplace vs rare, with precise tech tier. Name collisions between 'Relay' as protocol and physical hardware should be resolved.

## Three tiers
1. CIVIC — available baseline privacy and identity/consent systems.
2. PROFESSIONAL — specialized discreet access and correspondence systems, more expensive and supervised.
3. LEGACY/EXPERIMENTAL — discontinued or novel research with limitations and dangers, perhaps involving mother's work.

## Invention dossier requirement
Before approving any device: purpose, physical mechanism and speculative assumption, input/output, limits, needed permissions, safety/failure modes, price/access class, cultural effects, novelty relative to existing devices, impact on prior episodes, and story tension introduced. **No retroactive magic fix** for earlier conflicts.

## Potential mother-technology connection
Strongly appealing but unanswered: she may have helped develop a protocol or controversial experiment whose use in her final exchange had personal intent and far-reaching consequences. Do NOT presuppose secret murder, universal disaster, conscious AI resurrection, portal travel, teleportation or literal access to memories. *Fringe* is inspiration for science tied to family choice, not a plot source to copy.
