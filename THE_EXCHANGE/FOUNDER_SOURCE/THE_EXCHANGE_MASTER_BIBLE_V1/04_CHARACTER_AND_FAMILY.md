# Character Bible — Lockwood Family and Recurring Relationships

## SLOANE LOCKWOOD — NAME AND FACE SELECTED
**First + surname:** Sloane Lockwood, founder selected. Do not reintroduce Mercer. Approx age **mid-to-late 30s is a working suggestion, not locked**. Origin/hometown, education, languages, net worth and precise title remain open. Self-possessed, beautiful, sophisticated and striking with a charismatic, expressive face. Two files in visual_refs/ capture chosen face and character identity direction. Image typography from earlier AI collage (e.g. invented age, city, job) is not biographical evidence.

### Emotional center
Highly attentive to who is overlooked. Disarming, bright, clever, mischievous, competitive, sometimes petty, naturally charming, social and warm. Indirectly affectionate, afraid of accepting care without earning it, discomfort with being emotionally known. Has mastered arranging contact while struggling to be present when loved ones need her simply to stay. Father historically praised her utility and talent; she interpreted usefulness as relational safety without father intending to withhold love.

### Complexity and flaws
Jokes when nervous; precision and quiet when angry. Can be impatient, controlling under guise of helping, overconfident in reading motives. Wrong sometimes. She is not a human lie detector, universal polyglot, hacker, lawyer, scientist or all-purpose genius. Collaborators have genuine expertise and can refuse her.

### Suggested memorable quirks — WORKING_CONCEPT
Dessert first; handwritten thank-you notes; funny affectionate nicknames; hilariously poor directional sense; hates excessive voice notes; remembers staff names; can't abide imprecise stories; sentimental on birthdays; guilty-pleasure reality shows; perhaps inept gardening. These are options to refine, not all mandatory traits.

### Public vs private
Sloane may be socially bold, a wonderful dancer and warm friend in public parties, but cannot easily say 'I need you' in private. The series must not introduce her merely as an icy shadowy operator or use her as a device to explain technology.

## NADINE LOCKWOOD — OLDER SISTER, WORKING NAME
Nadine is a suggested first name repeatedly used in development and broadly accepted as shorthand; exact name, career, partner and children NOT locked. Older, grounded, warm, outspoken, loving, protective, sometimes controlling. Was given dependability and responsibility while Sloane got exciting work opportunities. Remembers home life and missed promises while Sloane remembers their father's magic. She loves their father yet resents cost to their mother and worries about Sloane repeating the pattern. Not villain/saint; sisters interpret same childhood differently.

## FATHER — ALIVE, NAME OPEN
Living charismatic, funny, socially gifted father, retired from facilitating exchanges, still loving confidant/mentor to Sloane. Generous and genuinely affectionate but formerly inconsistently available. His repeated 'What would I do without you?' rewarded young Sloane for competence while Nadine watched the cost. He taught Sloane connections, listening and negotiation; her success can surpass his knowledge in newer technologies. Retired in aftermath of wife's death linked to final exchange. He and Nadine avoid fully confronting the loss; some father-daughter conversations are warm and hilarious, not endless foreshadowing. May have regretted prioritizing obligations before family. Avoid unequivocal villain and magical all-knowing father.

## MOTHER — DECEASED IN PRESENT, NAME OPEN
Not a plot device; warm, brilliant, mischievous, morally articulate and an active partner in early network ethics/technological development. Empathy and design conviction. Her death is linked to a final exchange which she chose to engage with for a deeply personal reason. Exact goal, mechanism, technology, recipients, motive, success/failure, death circumstances and degree of informed risk all deliberately **UNRESOLVED**. Her husband left active Exchange work in the aftermath. Nadine sees systemic family costs; Sloane admires maternal generosity and legacy. Do not fetishize dying for loved ones as proof of love. Give her agency, flaws, joyful scenes, work, and relationships through earned flashbacks.

## FAMILY WOUND
Sloane: 'When I'm useful, Dad notices me'; Nadine: 'When everyone needs something, my feelings come last.' Both were loved; impact differs from parental intent. A proposed childhood waiting incident, important missed event and sisters waiting together are **illustrative** and unconfirmed. The mother’s death need not be murder or imply science disaster. The family can love each other fully while avoiding a difficult conversation.

## FUTURE ROMANTIC COLLABORATOR — NAME, CAST, ROLE OPEN
Introduced as skilled independent collaborator on authentic exchange; reappears intermittently, develops rapport, humor and complementary expertise, then mutual slow-burn affection. Not a savior, 'defense breaker' via pressure, omnipotent wealthy rescuer, or person who magically fixes trauma. He is interested in Sloane beyond her usefulness and has an independent life/arc. Consistency and trust create safety. Romance should have a satisfying, sustainable happy direction instead of cyclical artificial breakups; appears only after audiences have grown attached to Sloane herself.
