# Institution Bible — The Exchange, The Quiet Network

## Three-layer separation — proposed and strongly developed
**Quiet Network** = trust and authorized-access infrastructure / relational graph; **The Exchange** = discrete practice/institution that brokers ethical human and technological arrangements; **Sloane Lockwood** = rare trusted facilitator able to accept/decline cases. Sloane does not own society-wide privacy rights, cannot override protections and cannot compel third parties.

## Access routes
**Ordinary referral:** respected sponsor vouches for a petitioner, credibility reviewed, private submission and invitation if accepted. **The Recall:** rare high-priority contact concerning an existing obligation or disputed settlement, often suitable for 2 a.m. scenes, not automatic power to command her. **The Breach:** extraordinary contact bypassing established channels; investigate plausibility, never automatically assume network is supernatural. Rich and ordinary citizens may have access through genuine trust or historical obligation; money alone insufficient.

## The exchange lifecycle — WORKING_CONCEPT
1. Originating need and proposed outcome.
2. Sponsor / eligible referral (or extraordinary access exception).
3. Ethical/technical feasibility and consent review.
4. Offer: service fee or legitimate consideration; money is not identical with underlying human value.
5. Consented terms and ledger entry.
6. Facilitated encounter, disclosure, access or technical experience.
7. Settlement, modification, closure or disputed Recall.
Not every episode depicts all steps. Some narratives begin at consequences from an earlier arrangement.

## Sloane's code — proposed principles, not yet final legal text
- No coercion of a third party; participating individuals retain consent and exit rights.
- No offering goods, facts, access or rights the offeror does not legitimately control.
- Privacy/records not exploitative currency; no unauthorized exposure.
- Conditions must be comprehensible and modifications re-approved.
- Forgiven obligations stay forgiven.
- Refuse requests primarily intended to harm or exploit.
- Sloane maintains discretion to decline; membership is never automatic compliance.
This framework must be refined for people in asymmetric power relationships, vulnerable parties, minor protection, emergencies, lawful investigative orders and withdrawal mechanisms. Do not casually depict commercial sale of a human's consent.

## Economic model — DISCUSSED DIRECTION
**Fees** compensate technology, specialist labor, facilities, legal/ethics review and discretion. **Underlying consideration** may be voluntary, non-monetary and negotiated. **Sponsored/discretionary cases** create room for stories outside wealthy circles. The early line 'Sloane never takes money' is NOT hard canon; later concept favors separating operational fee from what the human exchange is actually worth.

## Infrastructure and governance
An elite but bounded mix of registry keepers, protocol/technology specialists, ethics counsel and external experts; no giant magical omnipotent staff. Sponsorship entails responsibility, not ownership of a client. Technology is authenticated but cannot verify sincerity or intentions. Access restrictions and rate limits, failures, revocation, ledger integrity, and auditability need design.

## Relationship to family
Father: living, semi-retired/retired former practitioner and adviser. Mother: pioneering ethical/possibly technological contributor, now deceased. Sister Nadine: skeptical of family cost, wary of Recalls. Sloane: professionally gifted inheritor with intense emotional investment. The final maternal exchange and the unnumbered/ancient obligation are exploratory mystery threads, not resolved case facts.

## Episode categories — optional expansion
Invitation, Unfinished Business, Seven Degrees, exchange; other possible tags include restoration, disclosure, protection, succession, witness, closure, retrieval, reversal. These are thematic categories not rigid repeated stages.
