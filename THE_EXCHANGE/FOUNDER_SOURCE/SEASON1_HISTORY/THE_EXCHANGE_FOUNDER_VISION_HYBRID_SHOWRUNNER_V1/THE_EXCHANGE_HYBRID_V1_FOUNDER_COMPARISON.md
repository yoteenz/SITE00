## 25 — Three-Way Creative Comparison

**Two biases to declare first.**
- **Resolution.** The founder's V2 is described in one page (Sprint 4 §5). WORD OF MOUTH and the hybrid are fully developed. Fully developed plans show both strengths and flaws. The hybrid *contains* V2 as its foundation, so V2 "losing" an axis is often about development, not ideas.
- **Authorship.** I wrote the hybrid's final version. An independent reviewer should test this table.

| Axis | Founder V2 (as described) | WORD OF MOUTH | Hybrid | Edge | Where the hybrid is weaker |

**Tally:** hybrid 7, WORD OF MOUTH 3, even 4. Given the biases above, read it this way:
- The hybrid is the strongest version of *the founder's* show.
- WORD OF MOUTH remains the most original and most producible *structure*.

**Precisely what the hybrid improves over V2.**
- A motive (§07).
- Living Legacy mechanics (§20).
- Defined collaborators and a defined betrayal (§09).
- A Vivienne truth (§10).
- A traced breach (§11).
- A complete map (§16).

**Precisely what it improves over WORD OF MOUTH.**
- The founder's glamour and joy are restored.
- The Grand Exchange is held and succeeds.
- The format is about a minute per release.
- LIFE and WORLD have a real share.
- Generational depth.

**The substantial weaknesses that remain.**
- **Production scale against unmeasured capacity.**
- **The load-bearing Ember decision (FD-05).** If it's rejected, §09 and §10 need a replacement shared creation.
