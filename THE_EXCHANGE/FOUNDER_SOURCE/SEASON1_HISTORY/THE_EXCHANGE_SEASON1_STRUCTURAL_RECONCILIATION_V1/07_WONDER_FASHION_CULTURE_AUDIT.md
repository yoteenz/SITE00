## 3. Inventions: wonder or protocol?

- **Each invention appears as an image first:** paper that blooms in one reader's hand, a line of light that runs a 1985 race, silence that becomes music, a friend waiting mid-smile on silk, a dryer ring that hums like a Saturday salon, lanterns that turn gold.
- **Consent is not every invention's subject.** The word appears in 10 of 86 releases, mostly in Arc 5, where the Ember's history *is* the story. Relay, the Echo Track, the Hush, the Interval and the Halo are about delight, privacy, memory and style.
- **No invention was added.** The Echo Track and the Hush were already in V1; Option A only gives the Echo Track a party around it.
