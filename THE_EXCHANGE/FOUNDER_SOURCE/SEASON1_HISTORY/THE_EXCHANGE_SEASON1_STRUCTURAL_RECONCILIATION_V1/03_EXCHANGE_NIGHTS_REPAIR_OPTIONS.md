## 1. The gap, measured

In BASELINE V1, an Exchange-hosted night (layer N) appears at S1-01 to S1-05 (the opening), S1-50 (*The Hush*), S1-67 (the rehearsal) and S1-68 to S1-80 (the Grand Exchange). **From S1-06 to S1-49, 44 consecutive releases, The Exchange hosts nothing.** Arcs 2 and 3 have no Night at all. Meanwhile other people's rooms carry the nightlife: the Crescent Room (S1-20) and Royce's Salon (S1-28).

**What a repair must achieve.** A night viewers wish they could attend, hosted by The Exchange, with Sloane visibly hosting. Not a networking function, an information session or a hearing. Placed so that Arcs 2 and 3 each get one, without inflating the count.

## 2. N1 — A new Private Exchange inside an existing release: *The Breakfast Dance*

- **Release and date.** S1-40, renamed *The Breakfast Dance*. Thursday night into Friday, GE−51 to GE−50, 2 a.m. to dawn. Prepared by one line added to S1-37 (*"Nobody should see a record fall without breakfast."*).
- **Location and visual.** The Riverside Pavilion, the river track's 1920s clubhouse: evening clothes at the wrong hour, silver chafing dishes at 4 a.m., then the whole party walking out onto misty bleachers with shoes in hand. A line of light runs lane four.
- **Why it exists.** Dee Whitlock's request (PX-07) is a dawn race against the girl chasing her record. A race at dawn needs witnesses, and Sloane decides the witnesses deserve a party. The night is built from the request, not bolted on.
- **Who is invited.** Dee's 1985 relay teammates in their old warm-up jackets over sequins. Zuri's teammates. Night-shift nurses coming off duty. People who never get invited to anything at 2 a.m. because they work it. Honora Vance comes for the race, not the party.
- **The extraordinary experience.** A party that begins when the others end. The band swings until four, then chicken and waffles, grits and salmon croquettes. At 5:40 the music stops mid-song and the guests walk out to the track. The Echo Track lights lane four, running Dee's 1985 record stride for stride. Zuri passes Dee and the light.
- **Patron requests.** PX-07 is the reason. Honora on the bleachers intersects the season arc (Kai's objection; S1-63).
- **Sloane's hosting role.** She hosts in a gown and carries her sneakers. She decides the guest list, stops the band at 5:40 and sits two rows behind Honora without pushing.
- **Cultural specificity.** The breakfast dance is a real Black after-hours tradition: a dance that runs past the clubs' closing time and ends in a breakfast. It also draws on a relay team's lifelong sisterhood, night-shift life, and soul-food breakfast as celebration.
- **Relationships.** Dee and Zuri become rivals who keep racing (*"Next Friday."*). Honora and Sloane share a bleacher without discussing Kai, which sets up the civility S1-63 needs.
- **Mythology.** The Exchange throws parties that exist for one person's wish, and strangers leave having been part of it. The Echo Track is experienced before it is explained.
- **Complexity.** HIGH: a night interior party, a dawn exterior, a crowd on the bleachers and a light effect. V1's S1-40 was already HIGH for the dawn exterior.
- **Additions, replacements, merges.** None. +0:45 on S1-40 (1:30 → 2:15) and +0:05 on S1-37.
- **Chronology.** S1-40's day changes from GE−50 to GE−51 to GE−50 (Thursday night to Friday). The order stays non-decreasing.
- **Mysteries.** Neutral. It gives Honora a human scene before her objection hardens.

## 3. N2 — Recontextualizing an existing gathering: *Nineteen Eighty-Six*

- **Release and date.** S1-20, renamed *Nineteen Eighty-Six*. Saturday, GE−70. Prepared by one line added to S1-12 (*"Clarence. I need your Saturday."*).
- **Location and visual.** The Crescent Room, borrowed by The Exchange for one Saturday: chandeliers, worn parquet, a photo booth that prints in faded colour. The band wears the white jackets it wore in 1986.
- **Why it exists.** Mavis Odom's Recall (PX-05). Richard owes her a dance, pledged the night Nadine was born, when she drove the family to the hospital in her fruit truck. Sloane decides that a forty-year debt deserves a room, not a phone call.
- **Who is invited.** Mavis wrote the guest list: forty years of market stallholders, her church choir, and the regulars who taught half the city to hand-dance. The family. Gideon is given the night off.
- **The extraordinary experience.** The dress code is *Dress like it's 1986*: big shoulders, bamboo earrings, pastel suits. Richard turns out to be king of the floor. Mavis dips *him*, the room roars, and Ada writes: *"Settled, with interest."*
- **Patron requests.** PX-05 is the reason. PX-08 (Mavis's hats, S1-51) is planted by the guest list. Noelle (S1-35, S1-74) is glimpsed by the bandstand.
- **Sloane's hosting role.** She hands every guest a dance card stamped with the year, wearing a 1986 cropped jacket she found herself. She cues Clarence and gives the room to Mavis.
- **Cultural specificity.** Hand-dancing, a big band, 1986 Black fashion, church-choir and market community, and a debt of honour paid in a dance.
- **Relationships.** Nadine reads the year on her card and understands this is about the night she was born, and looks at her father differently for a whole song. Richard is as happy as he has been in years, and says something to Bunny.
- **Mythology.** The Exchange can turn a debt into a celebration. Debts here are paid in joy, with a witness.
- **Complexity.** HIGH (was MED): a dance floor, a live band, a crowd and period dress. +0:15 (1:45 → 2:00).
- **Additions, replacements, merges.** None.
- **Chronology.** Unchanged (GE−70). S1-21 *Date Night* continues Gideon's night off.
- **Mysteries.** The whisper to Bunny still happens in plain sight. It now comes after the dance, not under it, which protects Mavis's joy, as Workstream D asks. **Flag (FD-08):** the disclosure now happens at a night Sloane threw. That deepens S1-55 (*"You decided for me"*) but makes her own party the origin of the breach. The founder should confirm this is welcome.

## 4. N3 — Moving the Unannounced Exchange: *The Hush*, earlier

- **Release and date.** S1-50 *The Hush* would move from GE−36 (Fri) to GE−56 (Sat), between S1-35 and S1-36, in Arc 3.
- **Location and visual.** Unchanged: a rooftop around an old water tower after midnight. Step inside the circle and the music is everywhere; step out and the roof is silent.
- **Why it exists.** PX-03: two radio rivals who have never seen each other each asked, privately, to meet the other. Sloane can't tell either one, so she throws a party.
- **Who is invited.** Strangers Sloane thinks should meet. The address is sent an hour before.
- **The extraordinary experience.** Hush pockets. Tad recognizes Yaz's laugh, she recognizes his voice, and they argue gloriously, then dance.
- **Patron requests.** PX-03.
- **Sloane's hosting role.** Matchmaker who denies it: *"I invited two people to a party."*
- **Cultural specificity.** Black radio culture (two rival booths broadcasting at midnight) and rooftop party culture.
- **Relationships.** In V1 *The Hush* happens on the same night as S1-49, when Gideon has been cleared and is *"loose and funny for the first time in weeks."* Moved earlier, Gideon's release has no cause, and the S1-49/S1-50 pairing (her tears in the car, then joy on the roof) is lost.
- **Mythology.** Unchanged.
- **Complexity.** Unchanged (HIGH).
- **Additions, replacements, merges.** None, but S1-50's opening line and S1-49's dependency must be rewritten.
- **Chronology.** Tad's and Yaz's referral cards arrive at S1-33 (GE−59). GE−56 leaves three days to plan an Unannounced Exchange. That is plausible but rushed, and it collapses the request's travel time, which the engine depends on.
- **Mysteries.** Harmful. *The Hush* is the season's emotional release after the breach suspicion (S1-44 to S1-49). Moving it removes the relief and leaves Arc 4 with no Night.
- **Verdict.** **Not recommended.** It fixes Arc 3 by breaking Arc 4. It does answer Workstream D's question for PX-03 (whether its Unannounced Exchange can help the gap): it already helps where it is, by splitting the second half.

## 5. N4 — A completely new venue: *Category Is* (reserve, C-11)

- **Release and date.** A new release after S1-35, at GE−56 (Sat), Arc 3. That makes 87 releases, or it replaces a request elsewhere. **Not added automatically.**
- **Location and visual.** The city's Museum of Decorative Arts after hours: a ballroom house walks categories between vitrines of historical gowns, under museum lighting.
- **Why it exists.** A ballroom house wants its thirtieth-anniversary ball inside the museum. The chief conservator refuses, because the gowns are fragile.
- **Who is invited.** The house, its legends and children, the conservator's staff, and Sloane's guests.
- **The extraordinary experience.** The conservator, who walked balls herself decades ago and never told her board, walks one category in white gloves.
- **Patron requests.** Its own request (C-11). It would intersect PX-02 (Tina, fashion) naturally.
- **Sloane's hosting role.** Broker and host. She persuades a conservator, not a crowd.
- **Cultural specificity.** Ballroom culture: houses, categories, chosen family, and archival couture.
- **Relationships.** New provisional characters. Light contact with the principals.
- **Mythology.** The Exchange can open any door in the city for one night.
- **Complexity.** VERY HIGH: a crowd, choreography, a museum set and vogue performance. It is the hardest release to generate convincingly with the current AI workflow.
- **Additions.** +1 release (87), +about 2:30. Or it replaces one scenario: its closest functional match is PX-08 (spectacle with eccentric joy), and that swap is not recommended.
- **Chronology.** Clean.
- **Mysteries.** Neutral. It competes with the Grand Exchange and the Living Legacy's gown vision (LV-2) for spectacle.
- **Verdict.** The most spectacular option and the best reserve for Season 2. It is not needed once N1 and N2 are in.

## 7. What remains weak

- **Every mid-season Night is request-driven.** No Night between S1-06 and S1-66 exists purely for joy. That is defensible, since the engine is the point, but the founder asked for legendary gatherings. A pure Exchange tradition (for example an annual night with no request attached) is the next candidate. It is not proposed here because it would need a new release or a replacement.
- **Two more HIGH releases.** Both new Nights are crowd and music releases. See `12_PRODUCTION_FEASIBILITY.md` for how to shoot them with reusable sets and controlled crowd geography.
- **Q10 (Life spontaneity).** The Crescent Room's Saturday is now arranged by The Exchange. *Noelle's Song* (S1-35), an unarranged afternoon in the empty room, becomes more important and must stay.
- **FD-08 context.** The disclosure now originates at Sloane's own party (N2). See the decision board.

**What the founder decides (S-3):** N1 + N2 (recommended), N2 + N3, or N2 + N4 at 87 releases.
