# 01 — Executive Editorial Assessment

## THE EXCHANGE · Season 1 Structural Reconciliation V1

> **NONCANONICAL. PROPOSED — NOT APPROVED.** This continues the Recurring Patron-Exchange Engine V1 (BASELINE V1, 86 releases). BASELINE V1 stays intact and recoverable. Nothing here changes canon, the Series Bible, the approval matrix, a repository or a production system.

## The three decisions that matter most

**1. S-1: why Sloane steps back, and how her season ends.**
- **Recommendation: Hybrid H.**
- The burden that changes is not the craft but deciding for everyone, a habit three generations of Lockwoods share:
  - Vivienne gave the Ember away without asking Celestine.
  - Richard told Bunny without asking Sloane.
  - Sloane excluded Kai without asking him why.
- She keeps facilitating impossible requests. The makers' choice (S1-75) stays as the visible consequence.
- The payoff is personal. Nadine asks her to dance, properly (S1-74). Then Richard asks the question nobody has asked her all year: *"What do you want, baby?"* *"The impossible ones. I still want the impossible ones."* (S1-80).
- **Cost:** three line additions in Option A, all reversible. **Dependency:** FD-06 (Vivienne's letter) and a shift of emphasis within FD-02.
- Details: `02_SLOANE_STEP_BACK_ALTERNATIVES.md`.

**2. S-2: how many releases.**
- **Recommendation: Option A (86) as the development master.** Option B's five merges (81) are pre-cleared as an optional production fallback, adoptable one at a time once the screen test shows what each release really costs.
- Option B cuts no request, no scenario and no request-to-payoff interval. That is unlike V1's sketched lean, which cut PX-10.
- Details: `04_EXPANDED_86_RELEASE_MAP.md`, `05_LEAN_RELEASE_MAP.md`, and the comparison below.

**3. S-3: the Exchange Nights gap.**
- **Recommendation: N1 + N2.**
  - ***Nineteen Eighty-Six*** (S1-20): the Crescent Room borrowed for Mavis's forty-year-old dance debt, with a 1986 dress code.
  - ***The Breakfast Dance*** (S1-40): a 2 a.m. party that walks out at dawn to watch a record fall.
- The longest run without an Exchange-hosted night drops from **44 to 19** releases, with **no release added**.
- Moving *The Hush* earlier (N3) only relocates the gap.
- Details: `03_EXCHANGE_NIGHTS_REPAIR_OPTIONS.md`.

## What improved

- **The Nights are back in the first half.** Arcs 2 and 3 each have a joyous, culturally specific Exchange-hosted night. V1's stress question Q11 moves from PARTIAL to PASS.
- **Sloane's motive has heat.** Under Hybrid H the season asks a question the audience recognizes: who asks the person everyone asks?
- **Four scenarios are sharper:**
  - PX-04: the family knows Delphine and chooses discretion.
  - PX-05: the dance is joy, not cover.
  - PX-07: the race gets a world.
  - PX-08: the hats get consequences.
- PX-01 no longer ties Royce's sponsorship to the toast.
- **Sloane is pettier and more human:** dessert on Royce's tab, and *"Dessert."* in answer to Gideon's real question.
- **Everything is traceable.** All 74 hybrid releases and all 86 V1 releases are traced through both options, and runtimes reconcile.

## What remains weak

- **Production is unproven.** Fifteen HIGH or VERY HIGH releases, mostly crowds and partner dancing, are untested on the current AI workflow. Recurring patrons have no face locks yet.
- **Wardrobe and inventions exist on paper only.** No wardrobe has been designed, the reference accounts are unreviewed, and the invention dossiers are missing (Q12 and Q13 stay PARTIAL).
- **Every mid-season Night is request-driven.** None exists purely for joy, and Arc 5's only Night is a rehearsal.
- **New costs:** Option A runs 1:50 longer than V1. S1-20 carries five jobs in two minutes. The breach now starts at a night Sloane threw (FD-08 flag).
- **Mystery load.** The central mystery is comprehensible beat by beat, but it asks a lot of the audience at once (T02).

**Stress test:** 32 questions; 24 PASS, 8 PARTIAL, 0 FAIL. Of V1's five PARTIAL outcomes, one improves to PASS (Q11) and four remain PARTIAL. Q10 is under slightly more pressure, and Q15 has improved.

## Recommended next creative choices

1. Decide S-1 first. It settles whether three Option A rows stay.
2. Approve or adjust N1 and N2, and decide whether Richard's disclosure at Sloane's own party (NR-5) is welcome.
3. Confirm the scenario roster (keep all ten, with four revisions).
4. Before any script: lock patron faces, test one crowd plate and one partner dance (S1-20's dip), and design Sloane's season wardrobe.
5. Decide whether one mid-season Night should exist purely for joy (U-2).
