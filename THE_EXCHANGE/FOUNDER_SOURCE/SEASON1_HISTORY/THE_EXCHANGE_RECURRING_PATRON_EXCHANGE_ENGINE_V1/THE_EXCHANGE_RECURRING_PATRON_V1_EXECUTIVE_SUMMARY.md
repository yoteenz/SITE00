# THE EXCHANGE — Recurring Patron-Exchange Engine V1
## Executive summary

> **NONCANONICAL. PROPOSED — NOT APPROVED.** This summary is a guide to the full package, not a substitute for it. Every decision the founder needs is in the main document (`THE_EXCHANGE_RECURRING_PATRON_EXCHANGE_ENGINE_V1.md`), the decision board and the revised release map.

### What the package does

The hybrid's Season 1 has a strong season story but underuses the thing The Exchange is for: people approaching it with an extraordinary wish and something meaningful to offer. This package restores that recurring engine across the season, alongside the four layers (requests, city life, Exchange Nights and the season arc), without replacing the hybrid's story.

### The ten scenarios

1. **PX-01 One Private Hour.** Royce wants the Crescent Room Orchestra in his phones-up club. It works, and he uses the night to toast her "last Grand Exchange."
2. **PX-02 The Woman on the Number 9.** A fashion editor wants to meet the best-dressed stranger on her bus. The stranger refuses the cover and offers a closet swap.
3. **PX-03 Off Air.** Two rival radio hosts, who have never seen each other, each ask to meet the other. A rooftop party under the Hush.
4. **PX-04 A Night Off.** The most famous voice in the country wants one ordinary family dinner. Potato salad.
5. **PX-05 One Dance, Owed.** Mavis Odom, a 79-year-old fruit seller, calls in a dance Richard promised her in 1986.
6. **PX-06 The Tuesday Table.** Gideon's mother wants to lose at spades, fairly, once. She doesn't.
7. **PX-07 Lane Four.** A record-holder says she wants to mentor the sprinter who will break her record. She wants to race her.
8. **PX-08 Fourteen Hundred Hats.** On her eightieth birthday, Mavis gives away all her church hats in one Saturday, each to the right head.
9. **PX-09 Sunset.** A beautiful public proposal that fails because nobody asked Renée what she wanted.
10. **PX-10 The Coat.** Sloane declines a request and the coat she has wanted for fifteen years.

### The decisions that matter most

- **S-1: what Sloane steps back from.** Read literally, the hybrid's step-back removes the activity the show needs her to anchor. Recommended: Alternative A. She gives up choosing the Living Legacy and being the only way in, but keeps taking impossible requests. "Just ask me properly."
- **S-2: 86 releases or a leaner version of about 81.**
- **S-3: the Nights gap.** No Exchange-hosted night between S1-06 and S1-49. This is the package's biggest weakness.

### The honest risks

No visual has been tested, and only Sloane's and Gideon's faces are locked. The cast grows by eleven on-camera faces and fifteen names to clear. The five fashion reference accounts couldn't be viewed. City life may begin to look arranged. Seven of ten requests succeed, so the failures (PX-09, PX-10) must survive any cut.
