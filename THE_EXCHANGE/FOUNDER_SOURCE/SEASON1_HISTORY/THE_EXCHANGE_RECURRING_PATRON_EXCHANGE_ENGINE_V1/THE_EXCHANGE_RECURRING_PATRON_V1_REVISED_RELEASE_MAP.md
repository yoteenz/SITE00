### How to read each entry

- **ID** is the revised release ID (S1-nn). **SOURCE** is the baseline release it comes from (LL-nn), or NEW.
- **CHANGE** is the disposition code: A unchanged, B incorporates an exchange, C replaced, D combined, E moved, F needs more time, NEW added. No release is coded G (redundant), and nothing was deleted.
- **FICTIONAL DAY** counts days before or after Grand Exchange day (GE0, a Saturday), with the weekday.
- **LAYERS:** R an individual request, L lives and city, N an Exchange Night or the Grand Exchange, A the season arc.
- Cross-references inside entries use the revised S1 IDs. Arc names are internal; public releases carry titles, not numbers.
