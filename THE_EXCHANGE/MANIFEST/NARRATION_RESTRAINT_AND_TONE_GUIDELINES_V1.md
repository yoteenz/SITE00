# Narration restraint and series tone guidelines

**Classification:** FOUNDER-SUPPORTED DIRECTION

## Narration restraint

See `SLOANE_NARRATION_AND_FREEZE_FRAME_GRAMMAR_V1.md` — silence when performance is stronger (grief, intimacy, vulnerability, family revelations, confrontations, decisive personal moments).

## Series identity balance

THE EXCHANGE must remain emotionally accessible and entertaining. Preserve balance of:

- Glamour  
- Humor  
- Romance  
- Social intrigue  
- Human relationships  
- Extraordinary Black cultural life  
- Sophisticated privacy  
- Meaningful facilitation  
- Family history  
- Institutional mythology  
- Mystery  
- Joy  
- Emotional vulnerability  

## Avoid becoming

- Technical institution explainer  
- Intelligence conspiracy thriller (default mode)  
- Conventional procedural  
- Corporate networking drama  
- Promotional vehicle for spinoffs  
- Over-complicated mythology exercise  

**Audience should want to inhabit the world.** People must remain more compelling than systems.
