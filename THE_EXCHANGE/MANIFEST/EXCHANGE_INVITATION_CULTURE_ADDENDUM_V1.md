# Exchange invitation culture — founder-supported direction

**Classification:** FOUNDER-SUPPORTED DIRECTION (not wholesale APPROVED_CANON)  
**Sprint supplement:** `P0.THE-EXCHANGE.CREATIVE-CANON-NUANCE.OPUS-PREP-SUPPLEMENT1`  
**Institution context:** The Exchange operates **between events** via the Quiet Network; invitation culture governs **who enters the room**, not the entirety of facilitation work.

## Desirability and reputation

Exchange Nights are among the most **coveted private social invitations** in this fictional world. An invitation **cannot simply be purchased**. Wealth, celebrity, title, or influence **alone** do not guarantee entry.

## Selection philosophy (proposed values)

- Extraordinary talent  
- Cultural influence  
- Intellectual distinction  
- Character  
- Potential  
- Interesting social chemistry  
- Emerging achievement  
- Unusual perspectives  
- Trusted personal relationships  
- Meaningful opportunities for connection  

Some of the most interesting people present may be **relatively unknown**; Sloane may recognize potential before broader visibility — central to institutional social philosophy.

## Proposed invitation pathways (pending reconciliation with institution bible)

| Pathway | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **A. Personal selection** | Sloane personally identifies someone she wishes to bring into the room. |
| **B. Quiet scouting** | The Exchange recognizes emerging individuals whose potential may exceed current visibility. |
| **C. Trusted introduction** | An existing participant recommends someone, subject to Exchange approval. |

## Operational tone (proposed)

- Invitations should generally feel **personal, selective, and nontransferable**.  
- Previous attendance should **not automatically** guarantee future access.  
- The experience must feel extraordinarily desirable **without** implying every attendee is wealthy or famous.  

## Explicit non-goals

Do **not** invent membership tiers, invitation pricing, subscription systems, or access privileges in this addendum.

## Relationship to The Exchange institution

The Exchange is an established, invitation-based **relational facilitation practice** within the Quiet Network — **not** merely a private club, elite networking service, or party brand. Exchange Nights **witness** philosophy in a glamorous, emotionally accessible environment; they do **not** replace facilitation work that continues outside gatherings.
