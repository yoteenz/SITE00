# Selective narration and freeze-frame language — proposal record

**Status:** PROVISIONAL storytelling technique (not locked format rule)

## Elements

- **Selective first-person voiceover** by Sloane Lockwood.  
- **Freeze-frame introductions** for pivotal characters: on-screen name + concise descriptor.  
- **Tone:** Witty, sophisticated, subjective private commentary; reveals personality and social understanding.  
- **Resume:** Scene continues naturally after introduction.  
- **Restraint:** Never overexplain; preserve silence during emotionally important scenes.  
- **Frequency:** Not required in every episode or scene.
