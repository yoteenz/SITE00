# Recurring guests and universe expansion — principles

**Classification:** FOUNDER-SUPPORTED DIRECTION / PROVISIONAL CONCEPT  
**Registry scaffold:** `RECURRING_GUEST_CONCEPT_REGISTRY_V1.json` (no invented names)

## Purpose within THE EXCHANGE

Recurring Exchange attendees may **organically** become pivotal characters. They should **first** matter naturally inside the parent series — **not** be designed primarily to advertise spinoffs.

## Emergence domains (examples, not exhaustive cast list)

Music, fashion, technology, finance, art, culinary culture, academia, entrepreneurship, cultural institutions, other distinctive worlds.

## Each recurring character should have (when introduced)

- Independent identity  
- Meaningful reason for attending  
- Personal stakes  
- Relationship to Sloane or the institution  
- Recurring behavioral characteristics  
- Independent relationships  
- A world that continues beyond Exchange scenes  

Some may eventually support standalone productions; others remain memorable supporting roles. Audience should **not** automatically know who will become important.

## Interconnected universe (franchise discipline)

THE EXCHANGE is a natural **intersection** of otherwise independent lives. Same character may read differently in parent series vs standalone — perspectives should **enrich**, not contradict.

Future productions must stand alone. **No** requirement to watch every related series for central Exchange story.

Long-term franchise potential **without** prematurely commissioning or canonizing spinoffs.

## Multi-perspective note

A guest may be charming at a Night, formidable professionally elsewhere, vulnerable at home, humorous with friends, or linked to another guest's history — design for **coherence across views**, not contradiction.
