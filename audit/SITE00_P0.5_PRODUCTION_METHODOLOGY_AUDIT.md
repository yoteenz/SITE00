## Headline Findings

### The factory is sophisticated but not coherent

Studio World contains **28+ production-significant systems** with strong types, gates, and lineage — but they operate as **parallel pipelines** rather than one production operating system. The GRAPHIC→PAGE branch (Experiment F, visual development, implementation contracts) is furthest along. PAGE→PRODUCT→WORLD remains contracts or absent.

### The single largest methodology risk

**Approved hero design proof → full site implementation** has no bridge. Without **page families, layout grammar, and surface-specific contracts**, Composer (when connected) would become the creative director for all remaining pages — reproducing either **one-design-copied-everywhere** or **invented page drift**.

### The single largest automation risk

**No dependency invalidation graph.** Upstream intelligence changes (Brand Lore, appetite, functional canon, references) do not reliably supersede downstream runs, proofs, or READY states. P0 made state durable; **stale truth can now persist durably**.

### Product Expression is correctly absent — and critically missing

Application intelligence modules exist in project intelligence intake, but **no Product Expression methodology** connects intelligence to product behavior, workflows, state, or implementation. Application projects would be forced through page/experience metaphors.

### Experiment F corrected concept collapse — heuristics remain

Experiment F + `conceptVsDirection` gate address the Experiment D parent-collapse failure. **Orthogonality V2 remains token-heuristic** — concept collapse could recur without model-level set audit.

### World Formation scaffolds are useful research — not formable worlds

`futureContracts.ts` defines extensive world domains but **missing blocker domains** (presence, economy, moderation, sync, UGC, session architecture). `WORLD_FORMATION_IMPLEMENTED=false` is correct.
